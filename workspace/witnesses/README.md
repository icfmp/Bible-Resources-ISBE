# Archived reconstruction witnesses

`crosswire-2.2/ISBE.xml` and `article-index.json` reproduce the CrossWire **digital** source as a stable source-locked TEI witness (SHA-256 `a54c060420518db1c15bbf54264ed3652ec45e62f93c9af106c44d101add5e25`). They are not a publisher original and must never overwrite the corrected editorial edition.

`print/1915-volume-witnesses.json` records exact SHA-256 and location of five historic printed PDF facsimiles used in audits. The actual PDFs are not stored in Git, and their text layers are not proof of complete print fidelity.

The original CrossWire ZIP and maintained corrected historical TEI live separately under `sources/original/` and `sources/cleaned/`.
