# AZNOTH-TABOR
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
az'noth-ta'-bor ('aznoth tabhor, "peaks of Tabor"): A place in western Naphtali, evidently in the vicinity of Mt. Tabor (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:34⟧). The exact locality is not known.
