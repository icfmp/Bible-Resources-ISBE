# BETH-PAZZEZ
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-paz'-ez (beth patstsets; Bersaphes, Baithphrasee): A town in the territory of Issachar, named with En-gannim and En- haddah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:21⟧). The site has not been discovered; it probably lay near the modern Jenin.
