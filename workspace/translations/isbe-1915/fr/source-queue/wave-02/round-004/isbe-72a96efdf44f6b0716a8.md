# CHASEBA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kas'-e-ba (Chaseba): The name of a family of temple-servants in the list of those who returned from Babylon (1 Esdras 5:31). The name is not given in the parallel passages in Ezra and Nehemiah.
