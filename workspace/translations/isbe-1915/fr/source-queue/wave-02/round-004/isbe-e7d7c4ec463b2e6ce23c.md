# BORDERER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bor'-der-er (parakeimenous): One who dwells on the borders or confines of a country. Only in 2 Macc 9:25, "The princes that are borderers and neighbors unto my kingdom."
