# BRAG
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(megalaucheo, "proud brags"): Occurs only in the Apocrypha (2 Macc 15:32), not being sufficiently dignified to be given a place in the canonical Scriptures by the King James translators (compare Judith 16:5).
