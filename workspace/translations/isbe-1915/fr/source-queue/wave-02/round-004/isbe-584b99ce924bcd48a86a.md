# BICHRI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bik'-ri (bikhri, "first born"; compare HPN, 88, 102): Father of Sheba who rebelled against David. Bichri is of the house of Benjamin and the word probably means a "descendant of Becher" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Sa 20:1⟧ ff). Compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧BECHER 1⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧
