# BUGEAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bu-je'-an, ba'-je-an (Bougaios): An epithet given to Haman in Apocrypha, Additions to Esther 12:6, the Revised Version (British and American); the King James Version has "Agagite".
