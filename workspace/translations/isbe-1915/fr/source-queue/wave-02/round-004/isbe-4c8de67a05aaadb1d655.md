# CHARGES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
char'-jiz (dapanao, "to spend"): "Be at charges for them" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ac 21:24⟧, the King James Version "with them"), i.e. pay the sacrificial expenses of these poorer Nazirites (compare Josephus, Ant, XIX, xvi, 1).
