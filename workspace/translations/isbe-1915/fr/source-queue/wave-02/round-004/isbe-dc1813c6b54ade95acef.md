# BEN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ben (ben, "son"): A Levite appointed to assist as musician in the temple service (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ch 15:18⟧). The text seems to be doubtful, since the name is omitted in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 15:20⟧ and not mentioned at all in the Septuagint.
