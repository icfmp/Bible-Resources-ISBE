# CARCAS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kar'-kas (karkac): One of seven chamberlains, ordered to summon Queen Vashti before King Ahasuerus (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Es 1:10⟧). The Targum allegorizes the first five of the names.
