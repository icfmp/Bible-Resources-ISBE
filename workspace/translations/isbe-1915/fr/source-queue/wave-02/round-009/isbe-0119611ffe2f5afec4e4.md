# BENAIAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-na'-ya, be-ni'-a (benayah, benayahu, "Yahweh has built." Compare HPN, 182, 265, 268):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) Benaiah, the son of Jehoiada of Kabzeel (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 15:21⟧), was a man of "mighty deeds" and was more honorable than any of the mighty men of David except the three chiefs. Therefore David made him his chief counselor (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 23:23⟧ m; month compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ch 27:34⟧ where the order of names seems to be reversed) and set him over the Cherethites (compare Carites, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:2Ki 11:4⟧ ff and margin) and Pelethites and he was made the 3rd captain of the host and chief over the course of the 3rd (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Ch 27:5⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:f; 2Sa 8:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:20:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Ch 18:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:2Sa 23:20 ff;⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:11:22⟧ ff). Being a true friend of David (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:2Sa 15:18⟧) he did not take part in the usurpation of Adonijah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:1Ki 1:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:26⟧), and was therefore with others chosen by the king to proclaim Solomon king over Israel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:1Ki 1:32⟧ ff) and later by Solomon to execute Adonijah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:1Ki 2:25⟧), Joab (⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:1Ki 2:29⟧ ff), and Shimei (⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:1Ki 2:46⟧). In recognition of his services Solomon appointed him over the host in Joab's place (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:1Ki 2:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:4:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:23:tail⟧
