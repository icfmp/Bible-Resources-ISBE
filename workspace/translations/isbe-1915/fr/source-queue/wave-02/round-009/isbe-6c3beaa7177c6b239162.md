# BUILDER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bild'-er (banah; oikodomeo, technites): "To build," "builder," ete, are in the Old Testament commonly the translation of banah, "to build," occurring very frequently; see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:3⟧BUILD, BUILDING⟦ISBE:CLOSE:3⟧. The literal significance leads also to several figurative applications, especially to God as Divine Builder:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(1) as establishing, e.g. the nation (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ps 69:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:102:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jer 12:16⟧), the throne of David (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ps 89:4⟧), Jerusalem (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ps 147:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(2) in restoration--rebuilding (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Isa 58:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:61:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:65:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Jer 31:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:42:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Eze 36:36⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:17:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Am 9:11⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Ac 15:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:10 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:20 -->
(3) as establishing in prosperity (⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Job 22:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:21:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:1Sa 2:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Jer 24:6⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Ge 16:2⟧ the Revised Version, margin, Hebrew, "be builded by her");
<!-- ISBE-END:20 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:25 -->
(4) the firm establishment of the Divine attributes (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Ps 89:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:26:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:25 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:27 -->
(5) Divine opposition (⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:La 3:5⟧, "He hath builded against me"); compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:Job 19:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:29:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:27 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:30 -->
(6) the choosing of a corner-stone which the builders rejected (⟦ISBE:KEEP:31:Ps 118:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:31:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:32:23⟧; quoted by Christ (⟦ISBE:KEEP:33:Mt 21:42⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:33:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:34:Mr 12:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:34:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:35:Lu 20:17⟧); by Peter (⟦ISBE:KEEP:36:Ac 4:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:36:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:37:1Pe 2:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:37:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:30 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:38 -->
In the New Testament Christians are represented as being:
<!-- ISBE-END:38 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:39 -->
(1) built by God (⟦ISBE:KEEP:40:1Co 3:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:40:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:41:16⟧) on Christ as the one foundation (⟦ISBE:KEEP:42:Mt 16:18⟧, on Jesus as the Christ; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:43:1Pe 2:5⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:44:f; Ac 9:31⟧ the Revised Version, margin; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:45:Ro 15:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:45:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:46:1Co 3:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:46:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:47:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:47:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:48:14⟧ (epoikodomo); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:49:Eph 2:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:49:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:39 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:50 -->
(2) as being continuously and progressively built up in their faith and life (⟦ISBE:KEEP:51:Ac 20:32⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:51:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:52:1Co 8:1⟧ the Revised Version, margin, "buildeth up"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:53:Ac 10:23⟧ margin, Greek "build up"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:54:Ac 14:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:54:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:55:17⟧ m; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:56:1Th 5:11⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:57:Jude 1:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:57:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:50 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:58 -->
(3) they are "builded togethe r" (sunoikodomeo) in Christ (⟦ISBE:KEEP:59:Eph 2:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:59:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:60:Col 2:7⟧ (epoikodomeo); compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:61:1Co 3:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:61:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:58 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:62 -->
(4) "builded up" is used in a bad sense (⟦ISBE:KEEP:63:1Co 8:10⟧ the King James Version and the Revised Version (British and American), "emboldened," the Revised Version, margin "be builded up");
<!-- ISBE-END:62 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:64 -->
(6) in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:65:Heb 3:4⟧ God is represented as the Builder (establisher) of all things, the Revised Version, margin "established," and in 11:10 as the Builder (technites), of the New Jerusalem; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:66:Heb 9:11⟧ for "building" the Revised Version (British and American) has "creation" (ktisis);
<!-- ISBE-END:64 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:67 -->
(7) in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:68:1Co 3:10-14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:68:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:69:Ga 2:18⟧, building represents constructing a system of teaching; Paul speaks of himself as "a wise master-builder" (sophos architekton).
