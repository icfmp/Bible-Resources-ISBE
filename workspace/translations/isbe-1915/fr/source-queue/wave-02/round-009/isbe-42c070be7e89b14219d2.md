# CHARGE; CHARGEABLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
charj, char'-ja-b'-l (from Latin carrus, "a wagon," hence, "to lay or put a load on or in," "to burden, or be a burden"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
Figurative:
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(1) of a special duty mishmereth, ("thing to be watched"), "the charge of Yahweh" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Le 8:35⟧), the injunctions given in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ex 29⟧; "the charge of the tabernacle" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Nu 1:53⟧); "the charge of the sons of Getshon" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Nu 3:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:9 -->
(2) of the burden of expense (kabhedh, "to be, or make heavy"; adapanos, "without expense"), "lest we be chargeable unto thee" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Sa 13:25⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "burdensome"); "The former governors .... were charge unto the people" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ne 5:15⟧ margin "laid burdens upon"); "that .... I may make the gospel without charge" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:1Co 9:18⟧; see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:13⟧CHARGES⟦ISBE:CLOSE:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:9 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:14 -->
(3) of oversight, care, custody, "Who gave him a charge over the earth?" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Job 34:13⟧); "to have the charge of the gate" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:2Ki 7:17⟧); "charge of the vessels of service" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:1Ch 9:28⟧); "cause ye them that have charge (pequddoth, "inspectors") over the city" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Eze 9:1⟧); "who had the charge of all her treasure" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Ac 8:27⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "was over");
<!-- ISBE-END:14 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:20 -->
(4) of a command, injunction, requirement, "He gave him a charge" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Ge 28:6⟧); "His father charged the people with the oath" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:1Sa 14:27⟧); "Jesus strictly (m "sternly") charged them" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Mt 9:30⟧); "I charge you by the Lord" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:1Th 5:27⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "adjure"); "having received such a charge" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:Ac 16:24⟧, paraggelia, "private or extra message"); "This charge I commit unto thee" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:1Ti 1:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:26:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:20 -->
