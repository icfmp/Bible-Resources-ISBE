# BASE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(1) Substantive from Latin basis, Greek basis, a foundation.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(a) (mekhonah): the fixed resting-place on which the lavers in Solomon's temple were set (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 7:27-43⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Ki 16:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:25:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Ch 4:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jer 27:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:52:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:20⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ezr 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Zec 5:11⟧ the American Revised Version, margin).
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:14 -->

<!-- ISBE-END:14 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:25 -->

(2) Adjective from French bas--low, or Welsh bas--"shallow": of lowly birth or station, of voluntary humility and of moral depravity.
<!-- ISBE-END:41 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:42 -->
(a) (shaphal, shephal): of David's self-humiliation (⟦ISBE:KEEP:43:2Sa 6:22⟧): "a modest unambitious kingdom" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:44:Eze 17:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:44:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:45:29:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:45:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:46:15⟧ (BDB); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:47:Da 4:17⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "lowest")): compare shephelah = "lowland."
<!-- ISBE-END:42 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:48 -->
(b) (qalah): men of humble birth and station as opposed to the nobles (⟦ISBE:KEEP:49:Isa 3:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:49:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:48 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:50 -->
(c) (beli-shem): "nameless," "of no account": "children of fools, yea, children of base men" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:51:Job 30:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:51:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:50 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:52 -->
(d) the King James Version men, sons, daughters, children of Belial; literally "worthless persons"; in the American Standard Revised Version "base," except ⟦ISBE:KEEP:53:1Sa 1:16⟧ "wicked woman"; also the English Revised Version of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:54:De 13:13⟧, "base," which elsewhere retains the King James Version rendering.
<!-- ISBE-END:52 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:55 -->
(e) (tapeinos): "lowly," "humble or abject" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:56:2Co 10:1⟧); the Revised Version (British and American), "lowly"; so Paul's enemies said he appeared when present in the church at Corinth.
<!-- ISBE-END:55 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:57 -->
(f) (agenes): "of low birth," "of no account" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:58:1Co 1:28⟧): "base things of the world."
<!-- ISBE-END:57 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:59 -->
(g) (agoraios): " belonging to the market-place," loafers, worthless characters (⟦ISBE:KEEP:60:Ac 17:5⟧): "certain lewd fellows of the baser sort"; the Revised Version (British and American) "certain vile fellows of the rabble."
