# BRANCH ;BOUGH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bransh: Represented by very many words in the Hebrew.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) zemorah used especially of a vine branch. The spies "cut down from thence a branch with one cluster of grapes" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 13:23⟧). See also ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eze 15:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Na 2:2⟧. "They put the branch to their nose" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Eze 8:17⟧), refers to some unknown idolatrous practice, as does also ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 17:10⟧, "thou plantest pleasant plants, and settest it with strange slips," or "vine slips of a strange (god)" the Revised Version, margin.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

(2) yoneqeth, literally "a sucker." "The tender branch thereof will not cease" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Job 14:7⟧). Used figuratively of Israel, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ps 80:11⟧ (the Revised Version (British and American) "shoots"); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Eze 17:22⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ho 14:6⟧, and of the wicked, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Job 8:16⟧ (the Revised Version (British and American) "shoots"), 15:30.
