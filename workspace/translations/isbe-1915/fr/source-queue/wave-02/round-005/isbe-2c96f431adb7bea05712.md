# BERITES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-rits (berim; according to Klostermann and others, bikhrim): The word is found only once in the Old Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Sa 20:14⟧). The passage seems to be doubtful. The suggestion of Klostermann does not improve matters any; the other proposed reading, bachrim (Vulgate, viri electi), "choice young men," is to be preferred.
