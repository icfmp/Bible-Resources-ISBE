# BETH-ANATH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-a'-nath (beth`anath; Bainathath): A city in the territory of Naphtali, named with Horem and Bethshemesh (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:38⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jud 1:33⟧). It is represented by the modern village Ainatha, about 12 miles Northwest of Cafed. The name signifies the "house" or "temple" of Anath, a goddess of the Canaanites.
