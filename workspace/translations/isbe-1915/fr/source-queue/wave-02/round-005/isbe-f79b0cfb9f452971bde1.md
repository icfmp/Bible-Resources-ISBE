# BIRSHA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bur'-sha (birsha`): King of Gomorrah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 14:2⟧), who joined the league against Chedorlaomer. The name is probably corrupt; some have tried to explain it as beresha`, "with wickedness," a name purposely used by the writer in referring to this king.
