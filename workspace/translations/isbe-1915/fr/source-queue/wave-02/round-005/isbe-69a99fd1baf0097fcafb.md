# BALL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bol (dur): A rare Hebrew word used in this sense only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 22:18⟧, and correctly rendered in the American Standard Revised Version "He will surely wind thee round and round, and toss thee like a ball into a large country." De Or, Bottcher, Jastrow, following Talmud, regard the noun as kaddur, but perhaps incorrectly.
