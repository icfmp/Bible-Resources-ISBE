# BETH-LEHEMITE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth'-le-hem-it (beth ha-lachmi): An inhabitant of Bethlehem, a town in Judah, 5 miles South of Jerusalem. Jesse is so named in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 16:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:17:58⟧, and Elhanan in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 21:19⟧. The children of Bethlehem are referred to in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ezr 2:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ne 7:26⟧; 1 Esdras 5:17.
