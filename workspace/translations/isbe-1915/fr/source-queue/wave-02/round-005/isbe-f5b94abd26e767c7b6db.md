# BETH-EMEK
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-e'-mek (beth ha-'emeq; Bethaemek, "house of the valley"): A town in the territory of Zebulun (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:27⟧). It has not been identified, but must be sought somewhere East of Acre, not far from Kabul, the ancient Cabul.
