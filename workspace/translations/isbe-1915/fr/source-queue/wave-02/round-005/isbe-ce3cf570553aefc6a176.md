# BATHSHUA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bath'-shu-a (bath-shua`, "the daughter of opulence" or "the daughter of Shua"; compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:3⟧BATH-SHEBA⟦ISBE:CLOSE:3⟧; for derivation see HPN, 67, 69, note 3):

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(1) In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ge 38:2⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ch 2:3⟧, where the name is translated "Shua's daughter," the wife of Judah.

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
