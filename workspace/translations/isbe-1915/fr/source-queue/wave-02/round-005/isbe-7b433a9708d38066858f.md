# BEACON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-k'-n. The translation of the Hebrew toren, which usually means "mast" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 33:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Eze 27:5⟧), but in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 30:17⟧ being used in parallelism with "ensign" the meaning may be "signal-staff" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 30:17⟧ the American Revised Version, margin "pole").
