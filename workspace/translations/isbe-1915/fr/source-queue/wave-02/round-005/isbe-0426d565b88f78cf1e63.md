# BETH-HAGGAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-hag'-an (beth-ha-gan, "house of the garden"). The place where Ahaziah was slain by Jehu (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ki 9:27⟧). The words are rendered in English Versions of the Bible "the garden house," but some take them to be a proper name. The location is doubtful.
