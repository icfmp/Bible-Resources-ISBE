# BEEROTH BENE-JAAKAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ben'-e-ja'-a-kan (be'eroth bene ya`aqan; the Revised Version, margin "the wells of the children of Jaakan"): A desert camp of the Israelites mentioned before Moserah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 10:6⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 33:31⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:32⟧ the name is given simply "Bene-jaakan," and the situation after Moseroth.
