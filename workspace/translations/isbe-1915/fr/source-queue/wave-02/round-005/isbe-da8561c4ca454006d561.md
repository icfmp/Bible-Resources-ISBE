# BETEN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-ten (beTen; Batne): A city of Asher mentioned between Hall and Achshaph (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:25⟧). Eusebius, Onomasticon places it 8 Roman miles East of Ptolemais, giving it the name Bethseten. It may be identical with the modern village el-B`aneh, but no certainty is possible.
