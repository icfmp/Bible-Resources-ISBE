# BOZKATH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
boz'-kath (botsqath, "stony"; Septuagint Basedoth): A town in the Shephelah of Judah named between Lachish and Eglon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:39⟧). It was the birthplace of Adaiah the mother of King Josiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ki 22:1⟧; the King James Version "Boscath"). The site is not identified.
