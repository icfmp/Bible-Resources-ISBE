# BOHAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bo'-han (bohan, "thumb," "stumpy"): A son of Reuben according to ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:18:17⟧. No mention is made of Bohan in the genealogies of Reuben. "The stone of Bohan" ('ebhen bohan) was a boundary mark on the Northeast frontier of Judah, separating it from Benjamin. Site unidentified.
