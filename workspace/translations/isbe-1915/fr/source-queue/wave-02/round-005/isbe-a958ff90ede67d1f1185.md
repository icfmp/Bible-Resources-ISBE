# BANIAS (1)
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba-ni'-as (B, Banias; A, Bani; the King James Version Banid (1 Esdras 8:36)): An ancestor of Salimoth. The descendants of Banias returned with Ezra to Jerusalem. The name is omitted (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 8:10⟧), perhaps due to the oversight of a copyist or a mistaken reading of bene, "sons of," for bani.
