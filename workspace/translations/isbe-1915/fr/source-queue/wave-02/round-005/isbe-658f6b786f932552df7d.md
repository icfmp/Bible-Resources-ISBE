# BEHOOVE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-hoov': Used in the New Testament for two Greek words dei (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 24:26⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ac 17:3⟧) and opheilo (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Heb 2:17⟧); the former referring to a physical, and the latter to a moral, necessity (Bengelon, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Co 11:10⟧). The former means "must," that is, it is required by the order which God has ordained; the latter, "ought," that is, it is required as a debt.
