# BRAVERY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
brav'-er-i: tiph'arah or `tiph'ereth = "beauty," "glory," "honor" and "majesty," hence, "splendor of bravery." "The bravery of their tinkling ornaments" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 3:18⟧ the King James Version), "the beauty of their anklets" (American Standard Revised Version). Compare bravado, bravura.
