# CALNEH; CALNO
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kal'-ne, kal'-no (kalneh (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Am 6:2⟧), kalno (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Isa 10:9⟧)): "Probably the Kulnia (Kullani) associated with Arpad and Hadrach, Syrian cities, in the Assyrian `tribute' list (Western Asiatic Inscriptions, II, 53, number 3); Kullanhu about six miles from Arpad" (HDB, I, 344, and 1-vol HDB, 109).
