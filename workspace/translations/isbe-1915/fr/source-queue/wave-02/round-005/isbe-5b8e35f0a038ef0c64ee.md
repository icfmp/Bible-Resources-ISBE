# BETH-SHITTAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-shit'-a (beth ha-shiTTah, "house of the acacia"): A place on the route followed by the Midianites in their flight before Gideon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jud 7:22⟧). It is probably identical with the modern ShuTTa, a village in the Vale of Jezreel, about 6 miles Northwest of Beisan.
