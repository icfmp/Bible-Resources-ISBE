# BEER-ELIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-er-e'-lim (be'er 'elim; phrear tou Aileim, literally "well of Elim"): Probably lay to the North of Moab, answering to Eglaim in the South (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 15:8⟧). It may possibly be identical with ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧BEER (1)⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧; but there is no certainty.
