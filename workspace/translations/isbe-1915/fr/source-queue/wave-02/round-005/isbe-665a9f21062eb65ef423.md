# BOCHIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bo'-kim (ha-bokhim): A place on the mountain West of Gilgal said to have been so named (literally "the weepers") because Israel wept there at the remonstrance of the angel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jud 2:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:5⟧). No name resembling this has been discovered. Given on the occasion mentioned, it may not have endured. Many, following Septuagint, identify it with Bethel.
