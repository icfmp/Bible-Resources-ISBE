# CANDACE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kan'-da-se (Kandake): Queen of the Ethiopians (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ac 8:27⟧). Pliny states that the name Candace had already been borne for many years by the queens of Ethiopia (vi,29). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧ETHIOPIA⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧. Her treasurer, "a eunuch of great authority," was baptized by Philip the Evangelist on his return from worshipping in Jerusalem.
