# BURIER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ber'-i-er (kabhar): "Set up a sign by it, till the buriers have buried it" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Eze 39:15⟧). "When the searchers found any human remains as they passed through the land, they were to set up a mark to attract the attention of the buriers, who followed them" (Dummelow's Commentary, in the place cited.).
