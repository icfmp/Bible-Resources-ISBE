# BEKA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-ka (beqa`, "half"): Half a shekel, the amount contributed by each male of the Israelites for the use of the Sanctuary (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 38:26⟧). Its value varied according to the standard used, but on the ordinary, or Phoenician, standard it would represent about 122 grams.
