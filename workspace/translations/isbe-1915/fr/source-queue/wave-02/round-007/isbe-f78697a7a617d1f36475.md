# BAALSAMUS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba-al'-sa-mus Baalsamos; (the King James Version Balasamus): B. stood at the right side of Ezra, when the law was read to the people (1 Esdras 9:43). Compare Maaseiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ne 8:7⟧). the Valley of Rephaim where David obtained a signal victory over the Philistines; it was higher than Jerusalem for David asked, "Shall I go up against the Philis?" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Sa 5:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ch 14:11⟧). The exact site is unknown, but if the Vale of Rephaim is el Beka`a, the open valley between Jerusalem and Mar Elias, then Baal-perazim would probably be the mountains to the East near what is called the "Mount of Evil Counsel" (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧JERUSALEM⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧). The Mount Perazim of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 28:21⟧ would appear to be the same spot.
