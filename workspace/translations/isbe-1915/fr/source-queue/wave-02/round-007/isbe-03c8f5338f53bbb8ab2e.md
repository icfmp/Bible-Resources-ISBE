# CALDRON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kol'-drun (the rendering of qallachath, cir, dudh 'aghmon) : Qallachath is found only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 2:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mic 3:3⟧. It is a pot for cooking, of undefined size and characteristics, in the former passage for sanctuary use, in the latter for domestic. Cir is translated caldron in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jer 1:13⟧ (Revised Versions); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Jer 52:18⟧ f(the King James Version); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Eze 11:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:11⟧. It was distinctly a large pot, employed both for domestic use and in the sanctuary. Dudh is translated caldron only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Ch 35:13⟧. It was also a pot for cooking. 'Aghmon is translated caldron by the King James Version in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Job 41:20⟧, but it is a mistranslation; the Revised Version (British and American) correctly has "rushes."
