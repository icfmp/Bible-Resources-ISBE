# BOUNTIFULNESS; BOUNTY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
boun'-ti-fool-nes, boun'-ti (haplotes, "singleness," "benevolence," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Co 9:11⟧ the King James Version; Tobh, "to be good" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Pr 22:9⟧); eulogia, "good speech," "blessing" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Co 9:6⟧); gamal, "to treat well" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ps 119:17⟧); shoa`, "to be liberal" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 32:5⟧); yadh, "hand," "power"): Paul speaks of the church at Corinth "being enriched in everything unto all liberality" (the King James Version "bountifulness," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Co 9:11⟧). The offering of the church at Corinth for the Christians of Judea is termed a "bounty," a blessing, liberally given: "and not of extortion" (the King James Version "of covetousness," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Co 9:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:6⟧). The word occurs also in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Ki 10:13⟧: "Besides that which Solomon gave her of his royal bounty," literally "according to the hand of King Solomon."
