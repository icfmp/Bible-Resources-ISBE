# BOOTY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
boot'-i (baz and baz): "Booty" is the translation of baz or baz, usually rendered "prey" and "spoil" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 49:32⟧); of malqoach, "prey," "booty" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 31:32⟧, "the booty--the rest of the prey," the Revised Version (British and American) "the prey, over and above the booty," baz); of meshiccah, "spoil" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Hab 2:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ze 1:13⟧; the Revised Version (British and American) "spoil"); of opheleia, "gain" (2 Macc 8:20). "Booty respects what is of personal service to the captor; spoils whatever serves to designate his triumph; prey includes whatever gratifies the appetite and is to be consumed" (Crabb, English Synonymes). Persons (for slaves, etc.) might be part of the booty.  See also ⟦ISBE:OPEN:7⟧SPOIL⟦ISBE:CLOSE:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
