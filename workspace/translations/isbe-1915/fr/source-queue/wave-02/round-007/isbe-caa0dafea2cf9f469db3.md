# BILSHAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bil'-shan (bilshan): An Israelite who returned with Zerubbabel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 2:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ne 7:7⟧). The name may be explained as the "inquirer" (new Hebrew and Aramaic), balash, the ("b" being an abbreviation of ben, as in bidhqar, and bimhal. Bilshan would then be a compound of ben, and lashon. J. Halevy (Revue etudes juives, X, 3)) translates the name "pere de la langue," 'abh lashon. In 1 Esdras 5:8, he is called "Beelsarus," which is akin to the form "Belshar" = "Belshar-uccur" or "O Bel, protect king." Bilshan points to "Belsun," "his lord."
