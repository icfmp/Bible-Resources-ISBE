# BURN; BURNING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
burn, burn'-ing: Figurative: In addition to the ordinary meaning, burn is used metaphorically in the following passages of the New Testament:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) kaio (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 24:32⟧), "Was not our heart burning within us," i.e. greatly moved.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
(2) puroo, used twice, once in the sense of inflamed with sexual desire (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Co 7:9⟧), "For it is better to marry than to burn" and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:2Co 11:29⟧ of the heat of the passions, here of grief, or anger, "Who is offended (the American Standard Revised Version "caused to stumble") and I burn not?"
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->
