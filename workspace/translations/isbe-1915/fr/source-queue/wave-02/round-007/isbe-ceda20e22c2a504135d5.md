# BASEMATH; BASHEMATH; BASMATH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bas'-e-math, bash'-e-math, bas'-math (basemath, "fragrant"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) Basemath, one of the wives of Esau, a daughter of Elon, the Hittite (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ge 26:34⟧; the King James Version Bashemath), probably identical with or a sister of Adah whom he also married (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ge 36:2⟧). Compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧ADAH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(2) Basemath (the King James Version Bashemath), another wife of Esau, a daughter of Ishmael and a sister of Nebaioth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ge 36:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:17⟧). This wife is also called Mahalath (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ge 28:9⟧), and is of the house of Abraham. Esau married her because his father was not pleased with his other wives who were daughters of Canaan. Compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:14⟧MAHALATH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->
