# BLAST
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(neshamah, ruach):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) The blowing of the breath of Yahweh, expressive of the manifestation of God's power in Nature and Providence. "With the blast of thy nostrils the waters were piled up" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ex 15:8⟧), referring to the east wind (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ex 14:21⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:2Sa 22:16⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ps 18:15⟧). "I will send a blast upon him" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Ki 19:7⟧ the King James Version; the Revised Version (British and American) "put a spirit in him," i.e. "an impulse of fear" (Dummelow in the place cited.); compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Isa 37:7⟧). "By the blast of his anger are they consumed" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Job 4:9⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Isa 37:36⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->
