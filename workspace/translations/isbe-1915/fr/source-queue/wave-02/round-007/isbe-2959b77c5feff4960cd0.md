# BAAL-MEON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba'-al-me'-on ba`al me`on; Beelmeon: A town built by the children of Reuben along with Nebo, "their names being changed" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Nu 32:38⟧), identical with Beon of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 32:3⟧. As Beth-baal-meon it was given by Moses to the tribe of Reuben (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 13:17⟧). Mesha names it as fortified by him (MS, L. 9). It appears in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Jer 48:23⟧ as Beth-meon, one of the cities of Moab. Eusebius, Onomasticon speaks of it as a large village near the hot springs, i.e. Callirrhoe, in Wady Zerka Ma`in, 9 miles from Heshbon. This points to the ruined site of Ma`in, about 4 miles Southwest of Madeba. The ruins now visible however are not older than Roman times.
