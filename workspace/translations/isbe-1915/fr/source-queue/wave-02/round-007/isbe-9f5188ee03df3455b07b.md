# BLESSING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(berakhah; eulogia): Sometimes means the form of words used in invoking the bestowal of good, as in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 33:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 8:34⟧; and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jas 3:10⟧. Sometimes it means the good or the benefit itself which has been conferred, as in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ge 27:36⟧, "Hast thou not reserved a blessing for me?" and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Pr 10:22⟧, "The blessing of Yahweh, it maketh rich." "The cup of blessing" (to poterion tes eulogias, a special use of the word in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Co 10:16⟧), means the cup for which we bless God, or which represents to us so much blessin g from God.
