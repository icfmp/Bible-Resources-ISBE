# BAALAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba'-a-la ba`alah; ("possessor," "mistress "): Three occurrences of this name:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
⟦ISBE:FIX:3:text⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧KIRIATH-JEARIM⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧ (which see) (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 15:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Ch 13:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
(2) A city in the Negeb of Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jos 15:29⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Jos 19:3⟧ Balah and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Ch 4:29⟧ Bilhah; perhaps also Boaloth of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Jos 15:24⟧. The site is unknown; but see PEF, III, 26.
<!-- ISBE-END:8 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:13 -->
(3) Mount Baalah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Jos 15:11⟧), a mountain ridge between Shikkeron (Ekron) and Jabnoel unless, as seems probable, the suggestion of M. Clermont-Ganneau (Rev. Crit, 1897, 902) is correct that for har ( = "mount"), we should read nahar ("river"). In this case the border in question would be the Nahr rubin. Here there is an annual feast held--attended by all classes and famous all over Syria--which appears to be a real survival of "Baal worship."
