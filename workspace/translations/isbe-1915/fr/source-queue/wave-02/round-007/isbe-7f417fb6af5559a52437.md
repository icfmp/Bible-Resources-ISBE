# BAGGAGE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bag'aj:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) keli, ("the impedimenta of an army"): "David left his baggage in the hand of the keeper of the baggage" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Sa 17:22⟧); "at Michmash he layeth up his baggage" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 10:28⟧). The American Standard Revised Version gives baggage for "stuff" at ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Sa 10:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:25:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:30:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:9 -->
(2) aposkeue: "Beside the baggage" (Judith 7:2), "a great ado and much baggage" (1 Macc 9:35,39), "the women and the children and also the baggage" (the King James Version "and other baggage"; 2 Macc 12:21).
<!-- ISBE-END:9 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(3) aposkeuazomai, ("to make ready for leaving," "to pack up baggage"): "We took up (made ready, Revised Version margin) our baggage" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ac 21:15⟧, the King James Version "carriages"), i.e. what they could carry--English: "luggage"; but others understand the term of the loading of the baggage animals.
<!-- ISBE-END:10 -->
