# BEER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-er (be'er; phrear; Latin puteus = "well"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A station on the march of the Israelites to the North of the Arnon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 21:16⟧). Here it was that they sang round the well this song:  `Spring up O well; greet it with song, Well, that the princes have dug, The nobles of the people have bored, With the scepter--with their staves' (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Nu 21:16⟧ ff).  The place is not identified.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) The town to which Jotham fled from his brother Abimelech after declaring his parable from Mt. Gerizim (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jud 9:21⟧). This may be identical with ⟦ISBE:OPEN:8⟧BEEROTH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:8⟧, which see.
