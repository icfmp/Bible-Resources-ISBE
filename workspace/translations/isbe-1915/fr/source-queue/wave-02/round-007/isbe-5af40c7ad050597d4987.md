# BEBAI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-ba-i, beb'-a-i (bebhay; Septuagint Bebai, "fatherly"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) Descendants of B: returned with Ezra to Jerusalem (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ezr 8:11⟧ called Babi; 1 Esdras 8:37); one of these is Zechariah, the son of Bebai (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ezr 8:11⟧, Zaeharias; 1 Esdras 8:37). 623 returned with Zerubbabel to Jerusalem (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ezr 2:11⟧; 1 Esdras 5:13; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ne 7:16⟧ gives the number 628); some of these had married "strange wives" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ezr 10:28⟧; 1 Esdras 9:29).
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:9 -->
(2) A chief of the people who sealed the covenant with Nehemiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ne 10:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:9 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:11 -->
(3) An unknown town (Judith 15:4). Omitted in Codex Vaticanus and Vulgate (Jerome's Latin Bible, 390-405 A. D.)
