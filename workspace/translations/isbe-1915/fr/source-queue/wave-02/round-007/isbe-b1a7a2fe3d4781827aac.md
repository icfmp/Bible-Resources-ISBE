# BOAST
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bost (halal, "to praise"; kauchaomai, "to vaunt oneself," used both in a good and a bad sense): To praise God: "In God have we made our boast all the day long" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 44:8⟧); to praise oneself, to vaunt (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 10:3⟧). In the New Testament the Revised Version (British and American) frequently translates "glory," where the King James Version has "boast," in a good sense (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Co 7:14⟧). In the sense of self-righteousness (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Eph 2:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ro 2:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:23⟧). Boaster (alazon, "a braggart") occurs in the King James Version (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ro 1:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Ti 3:2⟧); the Revised Version (British and American) has "boastful."
