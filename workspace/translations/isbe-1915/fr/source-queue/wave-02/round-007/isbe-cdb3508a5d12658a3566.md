# BUFFET
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
buf'-et (kolaphizo, "to beat with the fist"): Refers to bodily maltreatment and violence: "Then did they spit in his face and buffet him" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 26:67⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mr 14:65⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Co 4:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Pe 2:20⟧). Paul speaks of "a thorn in the flesh, a messenger of Satan to buffet me" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:2Co 12:7⟧). Used figuratively of self-control: "I buffet (the King James Version keep under, the Revised Version, margin "bruise") my body, and bring it into bondage" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Co 9:27⟧). The Greek in this passage reads hupopiazo, literally "to give a blow beneath the eye." In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Lu 18:5⟧ the same word is rendered "wear out": "Lest she wear roe out by her continual coming" (the King James Version "weary me" the Revised Version, margin "bruise me") (see Pape's Lexicon, under the word).
