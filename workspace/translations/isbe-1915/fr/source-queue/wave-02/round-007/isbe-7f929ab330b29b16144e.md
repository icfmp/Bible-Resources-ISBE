# BASTARD
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bas'-tard (mamzer; nothos): In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 23:2⟧ probably the offspring of an incestuous union, or of a marriage within the prohibited degrees of affinity (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Le 18:6-20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:20:10-21⟧). He and his descendants to the tenth generation are excluded from the assembly of the Lord. (See Driver, at the place). Zechariah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Zec 9:6⟧), after prophesying the overthrow of three Philistine cities, declares of the fourth: "And a bastard (the Revised Version, margin "a bastard race") shall dwell in Ashdod," meaning probably that a "mixed population" (BDB) of aliens shall invade and settle in the capital of the Philistines. In Heb (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:He 12:8⟧) in its proper sense of "born out of wedlock," and therefore not admitted to the privileges of paternal care and responsibility as a legitimate son.
