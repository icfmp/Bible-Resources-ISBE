# BELA; BELAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-la (bela`, "destruction"; the King James Version Belah, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 46:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(1) Bela, the son of Beor, was the first king of Edom previous to the kingdom of Israel and reigned in the city of Dinhabah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ge 36:32⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:f; 1Ch 1:43⟧ f). Septuagint Codex Alexandrinus, Balak.
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(2) Bela, the firstborn son of Benjamin (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ge 46:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:1Ch 7:6⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:f; 1Ch 8:1⟧). He was the head of the family of the Belaites (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Nu 26:38⟧), the father of Addar (called Ard, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Nu 26:40⟧), Gera, Abihud, Abishua, Naaman, Ahoah, Gera, Shephuphan (compare Shephupham, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Nu 26:39⟧), Huram (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:1Ch 8:3-5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Nu 26:40⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:16 -->
(3) Bela, a son of Azaz, of the tribe of Reuben, was a man of great power and wealth. His possessions reached from Nebo to the Euphrates (⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:1Ch 5:8⟧ ff).
