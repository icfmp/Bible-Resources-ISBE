# BOLSTER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bol'-ster: Found in the King James Version only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 19:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:16⟧, "Behold, the teraphim was in the bed, with the pillow of goat's hair at the head thereof" (the King James Version "for his bolster"), and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Sa 26:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:16⟧, "Saul lay sleeping .... with his spear stuck in the ground at his head." "Bolster" in these passages in the King James Version was used to translate a Hebrew word whose true significance is "the place of the head," or "the head-place." It will be noted that it has disappeared from the Revised Version (British and American), which rightly has throughout "head," instead of "bolster."
