# CARPENTER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kar'-pen-ter (charash; tekton): This word, which is a general word for graver or craftsman, is translated "carpenter" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ki 22:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ch 24:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ezr 3:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 41:7⟧. The same word is rendered "craftsman" in the American Standard Revised Version of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jer 24:1⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jer 29:2⟧ and "smith" in the American Standard Revised Version of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Zec 1:20⟧. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Sa 5:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:2Ki 12:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:1Ch 14:1⟧; and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Isa 44:13⟧, charash occurs with `ets (wood), and is more exactly translated "carpenter" or "worker in wood." Tekton, the corresponding Greek word for artificer, is translated "carpenter" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Mt 13:55⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Mr 6:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧
