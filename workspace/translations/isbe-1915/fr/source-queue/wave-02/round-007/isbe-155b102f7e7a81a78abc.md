# BY AND BY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
In the sense of "immediately" is the King James Version translation of exautes, of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mr 6:25⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "forthwith"); of euthus, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mt 13:21⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "straightway"); of eutheos, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Lu 17:7⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "straightway"); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Lu 21:9⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "immediately"). In English literally this meaning is obsolete (compare: "After you have dyned and supte, laboure not by and by after, but make a pause--the space of an howre or more with some pastyme") (Babees Book, EETS, 247).
