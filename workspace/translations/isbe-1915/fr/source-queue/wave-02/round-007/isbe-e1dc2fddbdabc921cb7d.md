# BETH-MARCABOTH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-mar'-ka-both (beth ha-markabhoth; Baithmachereb, "the house of chariots"): Mentioned along with Hazar-susah, "the station of horses" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 4:31⟧) as cities in the Negeb near Ziklag. It is tempting to connect these stations with "the cities for his chariots, and the cities for his horsemen" which Solomon built (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ki 9:19⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ki 10:26⟧). The site of Beth-marcaboth has not been identified, but Guerin (La Terre Sainte. Jerusalem et le Nord de la Judee, II, 230) suggests Khan Yunas , Southwest of Gaza, as a suitable chariot city.
