# BEHALF
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-haf': "On the part of" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 27:21⟧, i.e. so far as it affects them); "on the side of" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 36:2⟧). For huper, "over," in the sense of furnishing assistance, as in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Co 5:20⟧, "in the interest of Christ" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:2Co 5:21⟧); "for our good," "in his cause" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Php 1:29⟧); also, often in 2 Cor, in general sense of "concerning" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Co 5:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:7:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:8:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:9:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:12:5⟧). Huper does not of itself indicate substitution, although one who shelters ("is over") another, suffers "in his stead" (the King James Version ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:2Co 5:20⟧), as well as "in his behalf."
