# BOX
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
The passages in which this word occurs are ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ki 9:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:3⟧ (Hebrew pakh, "cruet," "flask," the Revised Version (British and American) vial) and the synoptic passages ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mt 26:7⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Mr 14:3⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Lu 7:37⟧ the King James Version (Greek alabastron). "Perfume-boxes" are mentioned in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 3:20⟧; in the New Testament passages the Revised Version (British and American) renders "alabaster cruse." "Alabaster" was a white stone much used in ornamentation; and out of it small vessels we re made for holding precious ointment. "She brake the cruse" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Mr 14:3⟧), i.e. the seal, not the vessel.
