# BUZ; BUZI; BUZITE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(1) Second son of Nahor (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 22:21⟧). The word occurs again in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jer 25:23⟧, by the side of Dedan (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ge 10:7⟧) and Tema (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ge 25:15⟧), and is probably, therefore, the name of a people living in the neighborhood of Edom. Buz and Hazo (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ge 22:22⟧) are probably the countries of Bazu and Chazu (the former described as full of snakes and scorpions), which Esarhaddon invaded (KB, II, 131).
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
(2) A Gadite ((⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:1Ch 5:14⟧) (buzi), "an inhabitant of Buz"), a title given to Elihu, the fourth speaker in the Book of Job (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Job 32:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧
