# BLOT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
blot (mum, contracted from me'um, "spot"): Occurs in the sense of scorn (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 9:7⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 31:7⟧ (the King James Version) it is used figuratively of a moral defect; the Revised Version (British and American) has "spot." Blot out (machah, "to wipe out," exaleipho, "to smear out"), to obliterate or destroy: "That a tribe be not blotted out" (the King James Version "destroyed," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jud 21:17⟧). To blot men out of God's book is to cut them off by an untimely death (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ex 32:32⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
Figuratively: "To blot out sin" is to forgive sin fully (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ps 51:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ac 3:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Col 2:14⟧). Not to blot out sin is to reserve for punishment (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ne 4:5⟧). The names of those who inherit eternal life are not blotted out of the "book of life" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Re 3:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧
