# BAAL (2)
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba'-al ba`al, ("lord," "master," "possessor"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A descendant of Reuben, Jacob's first-born son, and the father of Beerah, prince of the Reubenitcs, "whom Tiglath-pileser (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 5:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:6⟧) king of Assyria carried away captive."
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) The fourth of ten sons of Jeiel (King James Version "Jehiel"), father and founder of Gibeon. His mother was Maacah; his brother Kish father oœ Saul (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Ch 8:29⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:f; 9:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:36⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:39⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Sa 14:50f⟧). These passages identify Jeiel and Abiel as the father of Kish and thus of Baal. For study of confusions in the genealogical record, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:1Ch 9:36⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:39⟧, see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:14⟧KISH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:15⟧ABIEL⟦ISBE:CLOSE:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:16⟧JEIEL⟦ISBE:CLOSE:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:17 -->
(3) In composition often the name of a man and not of the heathen god, e.g. Baal-hanan, a king of Edom (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Ge 36:38⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:1Ch 1:49⟧); also a royal prefect of the same name (⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:1Ch 27:28⟧). Gesenius thinks that Baal in compound words rarely refers to the god by that name. See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:21⟧BAAL (deity)⟦ISBE:CLOSE:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:21:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:17 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:22 -->
(4) A city of the tribe of Simeon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:1Ch 4:33⟧). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:24⟧BAALATH-BEER⟦ISBE:CLOSE:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:24:tail⟧
