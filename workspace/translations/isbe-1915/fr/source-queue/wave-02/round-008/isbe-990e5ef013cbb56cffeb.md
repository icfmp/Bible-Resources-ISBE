# CARRY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kar'-i (nasa', nahagh): The English Versions of the Bible rendering of a number of Hebrew and Greek words, and it has several shades of meaning, of which the following are the most important:

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:16 -->
