# CARCASS; CARCASE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kar'-kas: The dead body of a beast; used sometimes in a contemptuous way of the dead body of a human being. The use of the word as applied to a living body is not found in either Old Testament or New Testament.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) It occurs as a translation of the Hebrew pegher, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ge 15:11⟧; this Hebrew word is also translated "dead body" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Nu 14:29⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Sa 17:46⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 34:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:66:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Eze 6:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:43:7:9⟧, and "corpse" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Na 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧
