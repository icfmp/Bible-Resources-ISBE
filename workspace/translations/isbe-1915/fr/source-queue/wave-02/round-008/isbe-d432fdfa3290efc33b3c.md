# BOSOM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
booz'-um: In the ordinary signification of the anterior upper portion of the trunk of the body, choq or cheq, "inlet", "lap" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 4:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Nu 11:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:De 13:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:28:54⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:56⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ru 4:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ps 74:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Isa 65:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:La 2:12⟧). "A present in the bosom" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Pr 21:14⟧): bribes carried ready for use in the fold of the robe. chetsen = "bosom" (with special reference to that portion of the body which is between the arms), occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Ps 129:7⟧; chobh = "a cherisher," hence, "the bosom" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Job 31:33⟧); tsallachath = something advanced or deep, "a bowl"; figurative "the bosom" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Pr 19:24⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Pr 26:15⟧ the King James Version). The Greek employs kolpos (⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Lu 6:38⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Joh 13:23⟧). For Abraham's bosom, see separate article.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:21 -->
Figurative: In a figurative sense it denotes intimacy and unrestrained intercourse (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Ge 16:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:2Sa 12:8⟧); tender care and watchfulness (⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Isa 40:11⟧); closest intimacy and most perfect knowledge (⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:Joh 1:18⟧); "into their bosom" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Ps 79:12⟧) indicates the bosom as the seat of thought and reflection.
