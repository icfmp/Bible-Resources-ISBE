# BUSYBODY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->

It is also found in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Pe 4:15⟧ the King James Version (the Revised Version (British and American) "meddler") "or as a busybody in other men's matters."
