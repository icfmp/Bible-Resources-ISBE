# BLEMISH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
blem'-ish:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) mum, me'um; momos: This word signifies no particular skin disease, as has been supposed; but is used generally for any and all disfiguring affections of the skin, such as eczema, herpes, scabies, etc., even for scratches and scars, as in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Le 24:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:20⟧; and thence for moral defects, as in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Eph 5:27⟧. The existence of a blemish in a person of priestly descent prevented him from the execution of the priestly office; similarly an animal fit for sacrifice was to be without blemish. In the New Testament Christ is presented as the antitype of a pure and ritually acceptable sacrifice "as a lamb without blemish and without spot" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Heb 9:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Pe 1:19⟧), and the disciples are admonished to be blameless, "without blemish" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Eph 5:27⟧). Rarely the word is used to designate a reprobate person (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Pe 2:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧
