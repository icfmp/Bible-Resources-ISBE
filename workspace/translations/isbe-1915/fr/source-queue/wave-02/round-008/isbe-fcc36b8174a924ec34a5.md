<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:1 -->
# BETIMES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-timz': In the sense of "early" is the translation of two Hebrew words:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) shakham, a root meaning "to incline the shoulder to a load," hence "to load up," "start early": in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ge 26:31⟧ "they rose up betimes in the morning," also in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Ch 36:15⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version "early");
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) of shachar, a root meaning "to dawn" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Job 8:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:24:5⟧, the American Standard Revised Version "diligently," and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Pro 13:24⟧, "chasteneth him betimes."
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
In the Apocrypha (Sirach 6:36) "betimes" is the translation of orthizo, literally, "to rise early in the morning," while in Bel and the Dragon verse 16 the same word is translated "betime."
<!-- ISBE-END:10 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:11 -->
In other cases the King James Version "betimes" appears as "before the time" (Sirach 51:30); "early" (1 Macc 4:52; 11:67); "the mourning" (1 Macc 5:30).
<!-- ISBE-END:11 -->
