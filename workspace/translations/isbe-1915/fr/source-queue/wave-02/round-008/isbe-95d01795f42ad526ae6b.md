# BINNUI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bin'-u-i (binnuy, a proper name, "a building up"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A Levite, living in the time of Ezra (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ezr 8:33⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ne 10:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:12:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(2) One of the bene Pachath-mo'abh who had taken foreign wives (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ezr 10:30⟧--Balnuus of 1 Esdras 9:31) and one of the bene Bani (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ezr 10:38⟧) who had also intermarried.
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(3) The son of Henadad, who built part of the wall of Jerusalem (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ne 3:24⟧), and sealed the covenant with Nehemiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ne 10:9⟧). In all probability he is identical with "Bavvai, the son of Henadad" mentioned in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ne 3:18⟧. "Bavvai" is either a corruption of "Binnui," or is the name of the Levitical house of which Bavvai was the chief representative. Binnui is mentioned in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Ne 10:9⟧ as a leading Levite, and, besides, the names in these verses are obviously those of priests and Levites; so the former theory is probably correct.  (4) Head of a family who returned with Zerubbabel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Ne 7:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ezr 2:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧
