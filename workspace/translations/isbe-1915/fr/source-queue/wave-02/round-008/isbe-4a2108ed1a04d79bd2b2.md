# BEAR; BORNE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bar, born (nasa'; lambano, anaphero, bastazo): In English Versions of the Bible the physical sense is familiar, of supporting or carrying any weight or burden.
