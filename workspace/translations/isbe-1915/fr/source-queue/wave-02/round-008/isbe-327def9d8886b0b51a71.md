# BROAD
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
brod (rochabh, "width"; rechobh, "a broadway," "street," "court"; eurchoros, "spacious"): Occurs frequently as a term of dimension (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 27:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 6:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eze 40:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:43⟧ the Revised Version (British and American), "handbreadth long") and as indicative of strength (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ne 3:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jer 51:58⟧). The centers of communal life are called the "broad places," often rendered "streets" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jer 5:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:So 3:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Na 2:4⟧). A court before the temple: "the broad place on the east" (the King James Version "the east street," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:2Ch 29:4⟧); "broad plates" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Nu 16:38⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:39⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "beaten").
