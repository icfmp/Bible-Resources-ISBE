# BOIL (2)
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(verb) (bashal, rathach): "Boil" is the translation of bashal, "to bubble up," "to boil," "to be cooked," Piel, "to cause to boil," "to cook" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Le 8:31⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 19:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Ki 6:29⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Eze 46:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:24⟧ bis); of rathach, "to be hot," "to boil," "to be made to boil," "to be greatly moved" under strong emotion (the bowels), Hiphil "to cause to boil" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Job 30:27⟧ the King James Version "My bowels boiled, and rested not," the English Revised Version "My bowels boil." the American Standard Revised Version "My heart is troubled"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Job 41:31⟧, "He maketh the deep to boil like a pot"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Eze 24:5⟧, "make it boil well"); of ba`ah, "to bubble" or "well up" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Isa 64:2⟧ (1, in Hebrew) "The fire causeth the waters to boil"); in King James Version, margin of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ps 45:1⟧ ("My heart is inditing a good matter") we have Hebrew "boileth" or "bubbleth up" (rachash, "to boil" or "bubble up," the Revised Version (British and American) text, "My heart overfloweth with a goodly matter").
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:13 -->
"Boiling-places," occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Eze 46:23⟧ as the translation of mebhashsheloth, "hearths," "boiling-places." The American Standard Revised Version has "boiling-houses" for "places of them that boil" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Eze 46:24⟧), "boil well" for "consume" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Eze 24:10⟧). the American Standard Revised Version has "boiling over" for "unstable" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ge 49:4⟧; the English Revised Version, margin "bubbling over").
