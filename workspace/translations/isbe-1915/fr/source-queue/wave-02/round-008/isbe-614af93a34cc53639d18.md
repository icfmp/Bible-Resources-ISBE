# BABE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bab:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) na`ar; pais of a male infant 3 months old (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ex 2:6⟧) translated elsewhere "boy" or "lad."
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
(2) `olel, ta`alulim, in the general sense of "child" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ps 8:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:17:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 3:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:9 -->
(3) brephos an unborn or newborn child (King James Version and the Revised Version (British and American) of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Lu 1:41⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:44⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:2:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:1Pe 2:2⟧ and the Revised Version (British and American) of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Lu 18:15⟧ [AV "infants"]; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ac 7:19⟧ [King James Version, "young children"] and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:2Ti 3:15⟧ [King James Version, "child"]).
<!-- ISBE-END:9 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:18 -->
(4) nepios = (Latin infans) "a child that cannot speak." (King James and the Revised Version (British and American) of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Mt 11:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:21:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:20:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Lu 10:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:21:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Ro 2:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:1Co 3:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:23:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Heb 5:13⟧) the same word is translated "child," plural "children" (in the King James Version and the Revised Version (British and American) of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:1Co 13:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:25:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Ga 4:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:26:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:27:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:27:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:Eph 4:14⟧) the verb nepiazete is translated in the King James Version "be ye children" and in the Revised Version (British and American) "be ye babes" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:1Co 14:20⟧). Nepios is used metaphorically of those who are like children, of simple and single minds, as opposed to the "wise and understanding" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:30:Mt 11:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:30:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:31:Lu 10:21⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:32⟧1Co 14:20⟦ISBE:CLOSE:32⟧). "Babes in Christ" are men of little spiritual growth, carnal as opposed to spiritual (⟦ISBE:KEEP:33:1Co 3:1⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:34⟧Heb 5:13; Eph 4:14⟦ISBE:CLOSE:34⟧). Nepios is also used of a child as a minor or infant in the eye of the law (⟦ISBE:KEEP:35:Ga 4:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:35:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:36:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:36:tail⟧
