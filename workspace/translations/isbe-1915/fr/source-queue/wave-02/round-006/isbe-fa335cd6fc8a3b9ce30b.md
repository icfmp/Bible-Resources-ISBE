# BIGTHAN; BIGTHANA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
big'-than big-tha'-na (bighethan, bighethana'; Septuagint omits name): One of the two chamberlains or eunuchs of Xerxes on ("Ahasuerus") who conspired against the king's life, the conspiracy being detected by Mordecai and the culprits hanged (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Es 2:21⟧). Possibly these men had been partially superseded by the degradation of Vashti and were thus prompted to take revenge Xerxes.
