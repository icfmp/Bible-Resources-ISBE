# CHARITABLY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
char'-i-ta-bli (kata agapen): The Revised Version (British and American), which substitutes "love" for "charity" regularly, removing the latter word from the vocabulary of Scripture, makes a like change in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ro 14:15⟧, the only occurrence of "charitably" in the King James Version; the Revised Version (British and American) "in love."
