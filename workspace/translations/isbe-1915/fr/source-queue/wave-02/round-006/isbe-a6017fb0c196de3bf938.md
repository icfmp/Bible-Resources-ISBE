# BODY OF HEAVEN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
The King James Version translates the Hebrew idiom, etsem ha-shamayim, by "the body of heaven" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 24:10⟧). A more correct rendering is given in the Revised Version (British and American), "the very heaven," taking the word `etsem in its idiomatic use as an intensive, which is derived from its literal meaning, "bone," as "strength," "substance," and then as "self" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 21:23⟧); the substance of the blue, unclouded sky, hence, the clear sky itself.
