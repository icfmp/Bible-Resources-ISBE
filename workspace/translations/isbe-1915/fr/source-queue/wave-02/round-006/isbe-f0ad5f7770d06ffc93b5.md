# BECK; BECKON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bek, bek'-'-n (neuma): This word from neuo, "to nod," "beckon," "make a sign" by moving the head or eyes (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 5:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Joh 13:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ac 21:40⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:24:10⟧), occurs only in 2 Macc 8:18, "Almighty God who at a beck can cast down both them that come against us, and also all the world," the Revised Version (British and American), "able at a beck." So Shak, "troops of soldiers at their beck"; "nod" is now generally used.
