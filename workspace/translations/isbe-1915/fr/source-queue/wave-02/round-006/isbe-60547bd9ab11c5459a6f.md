# BY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
In the sense of "against" which survives only in dialectal English (compare Wright, Dialect Dict., I, 470, for examples) is the King James Version rendering of the dative emauto of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Co 4:4⟧ (the American Standard Revised Version renders this "against"). In classical Greek the same idiom sunoida with dative = "be conscious" or "be cognizant of" a thing.
