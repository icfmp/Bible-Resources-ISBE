# CABUL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ka'-bul (kabhul; Codex Vaticanus, Chobamasomel; Codex Alexandrinus, Chabol apo aristeron):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A city on the boundary between Asher and Zebulun (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 19:27⟧). It corresponds to the Chabolo of Josephus (Vita, 43, etc.), and is represented by the modern village Kabul, about nine miles Southeast of Acre.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
(2) A district probably connected with (1), containing 20 cities, given by Solomon to Hiram king of Tyre (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ki 9:10⟧ ff).
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->
