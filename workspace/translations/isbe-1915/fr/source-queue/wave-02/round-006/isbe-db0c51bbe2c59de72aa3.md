# BETH-AVEN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-a'-ven (beth 'awen; Baithon, Baithaun): A place on the northern boundary of the territory of Benjamin (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 18:12⟧) East of Bethel, near Ai (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 7:2⟧), West of Michmash (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Sa 13:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:14:23⟧). Beth-aven, "house of vanity," i.e. "idolatry," may possibly represent an original beth-'on, "house of wealth." Wilson (PEFS, 1869, 126) suggests Khirbet An, West of Michmash. The name is used in mockery for Bethel by Hosea (4:15; 10:5,8, etc.; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Am 5:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
