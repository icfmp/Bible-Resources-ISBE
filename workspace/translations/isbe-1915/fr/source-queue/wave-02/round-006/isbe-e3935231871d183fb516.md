# BRAWLER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
brol'-er (Qere midhyanim; Kethibh midhwanim, "quarrelsomeness"; amachos, "not fighting"): Spoken of the quarrelsome woman; "a contentious (the King James Version "brawling") woman" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 21:9⟧). He who seeks the office of a bishop should be "no brawler" (paroinos the King James Version "given to wine," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Tit 1:7⟧); "not contentious" (the King James Version "not a brawler," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ti 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Tit 3:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧
