# BETH-DIBLATHAIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-dib-la-tha'-im (beth dibhlathayim; oikos Deblaithaim, literally, "house of Diblathaim"): A town in Moab mentioned with Dibon and Nebo (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 48:22⟧). It is probably identical with Almondiblathaim (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 33:46⟧ f). Mesha claims to have fortified it along with Mehedeba and Ba`al-me`on (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧MOABITE STONE⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧). The place is not yet identified.
