# BAAL-ZEPHON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba-al-ze'-fon ba`al tsephon; Beelsepphon; (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 14:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Nu 33:7⟧): The name means "Lord of the North," and the place was opposite the Hebrew camp, which was between Migdol and the sea. It may have been the shrine of a Semitic deity, but the position is unknown (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧EXODUS⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧). Goodwin (see Brugsch, Hist. Egt., II, 363) found the name Baali-Zapuna as that of a god mentioned in an Egyptian papyrus in the British Museum.
