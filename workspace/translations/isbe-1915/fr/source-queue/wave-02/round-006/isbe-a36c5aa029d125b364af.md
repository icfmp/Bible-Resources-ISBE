# CHAMBERS OF IMAGERY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
im'-aj-ri, im'-a-jer-i (maskith): The reference (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Eze 8:12⟧) is to chambers in the temple where the elders of Israel were wont to assemble and practice rites of an idolatrous character. What the imagery consisted of, we may gather from 8:10: symbolic representations of beasts and reptiles and "detestable things." It is thought that these symbols were of a zodiacal character. The worship of the planets was in vogue at the time of the prophet among the degenerate Israelites.
