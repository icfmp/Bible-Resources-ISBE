# BLESSING, CUP OF
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(to poterion tes eulogias, "the consecrated cup," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Co 10:16⟧): A technical term from the Jewish liturgy transferred to the Lord's Supper, and signifying the cup of wine upon which a blessing was pronounced. The suggestion that it carries with it a higher significance, as a cup that brings blessing, is not without force. The succeeding words, "we bless," are equivalent to "for which we give thanks." It was consecrated by thanksgiving and prayer.
