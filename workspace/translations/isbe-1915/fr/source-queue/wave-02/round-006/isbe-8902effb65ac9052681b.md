# BUKKI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
buk'-i (buqqi, "mouth of Jah"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A Danite, son of the tribal prince Jogli (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 34:22⟧); he was one of the representative chiefs who assisted in the division of the land.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
(2) Son of Abishua and father of Izzi, a priest, fourth in descent from Aaron, in the line of Eleazar (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ch 6:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:51⟧), and ancestor of Ezra (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ezr 7:4⟧). In 2 Esdras 1:2 the name appears as Borith, and in 1 Esdras 8:2 as Boccas.
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->
