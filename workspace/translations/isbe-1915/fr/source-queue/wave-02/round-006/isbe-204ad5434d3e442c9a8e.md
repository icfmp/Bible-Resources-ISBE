# BLESSEDNESS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bles'-ed-nes: This translation of makarismos (a word signifying "beatification" or "the ascription of blessing"), is used but three times, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ro 4:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:9⟧, and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ga 4:15⟧, in the King James Version only. In the first two instances it refers to the happy state or condition of a man to whom Christ's righteousness is imputed by faith, and in the last to a man's experience of that condition.
