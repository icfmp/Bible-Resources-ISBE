# BARACHIAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bar-a-ki'-a (Barachias; the King James Version Barachias; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 23:35⟧): Father of Zachariah who was murdered between the sanctuary and the altar. It is possible that reference is made to Zechariah, the son of Jehoiada (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ch 24:20⟧ ff), whom Matthew by mistake calls "Z., the son of Barachiah." ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Lu 11:51⟧ omits the name of the father of Z. (compare Zahn's Kommentar, 649, note).
