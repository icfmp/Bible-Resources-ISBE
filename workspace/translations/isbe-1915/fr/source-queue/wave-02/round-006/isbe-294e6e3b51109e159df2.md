# CAESAR
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
se'-zar (Kaisar): Originally the surname of the Julian gens (thus, Caius Julius Caesar); afterward a name borne by the Roman emperors. In the New Testament the name is definitely applied to Augustus (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 2:1⟧, "Caesar Augustus"), to whom it belonged by adoption, and to Tiberius (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 3:1⟧, "Tiberius Caesar"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mt 22:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:21⟧). The "Caesar" to whom Paul appealed (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ac 25:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:21⟧) was Nero. The form is perpetuated in "Kaiser" and "Czar."
