# BETH-CAR
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth'-kar (beth-kar; Baithchor, Belchor): "And the men of Israel went out of Mizpah, and pursued the Philistines, and smote them, Until they came under Beth-car" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 7:11⟧). `Ain Karem has been suggested; if Mizpah is nebi Samwil then this identification is probable, as the pursuit would be along the deep Wady beit Hannineh--a natural line of retreat for the Philistines to take.
