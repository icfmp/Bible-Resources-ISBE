<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:1 -->
# BELIE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-li': Is the translation of kachash, "to be untrue" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 5:12⟧), "They have belied the Lord" (the American Standard Revised Version "denied Yahweh"), here used as synonym of "give the lie to."
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
In The Wisdom of Solomon 1:11 "belle" translates katapseudomai (the kata prefix referring to the kata in katalalia in the same verse), "A mouth that belieth destroyeth a soul."
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->
