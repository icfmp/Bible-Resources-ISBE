# BATH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(bath): A liquid measure equal to about 9 gallons, English measure. It seems to have been regarded as a standard for liquid measures (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Eze 45:10⟧), as in the case of the molten sea and the lavers in Solomon's temple (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 7:26⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:38⟧), and for measuring oil and wine (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:2Ch 2:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ezr 7:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 5:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Eze 45:14⟧). Its relation to the homer is given in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Eze 45:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧
