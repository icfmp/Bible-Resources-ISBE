# CALCOL; CHALKOL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kal'-kol, kal'-kol (kalkol): Mentioned in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 4:31⟧ as one of the wise men with whom Solomon was compared. The better orthography is Calcol which the King James Version gives for the same name in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 2:6⟧. In the former passage, Calcol is the son of Mahol, while in the latter he is called the son of Zerah of the tribe of Judah, and a brother of Heman and Ethan.
