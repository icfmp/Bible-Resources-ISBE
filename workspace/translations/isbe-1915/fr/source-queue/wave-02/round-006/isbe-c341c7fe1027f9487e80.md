# BEFORE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be-for': The translation of a great variety of Hebrew and Greek words. "Haran died before (the English Revised Version "in the presence of," literally "before the face of") his father Terah" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 11:28⟧). To be "before" God is to enjoy His favor (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 31:22⟧). "The Syrians before" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 9:12⟧ the Revised Version, margin "on the east," as "behind," owing to the position of Canaan, relative to Syria, implies the west).
