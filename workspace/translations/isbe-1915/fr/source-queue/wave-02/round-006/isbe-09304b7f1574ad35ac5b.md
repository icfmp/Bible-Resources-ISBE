# BRIDE-CHAMBER, SONS (CHILDREN) OF THE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(hoi huioi tou numphonos): These were friends or companions of the bridegroom and were usually very numerous (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 9:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mr 2:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Lu 5:34⟧). Any wedding guest might be included in the expression, or anyone who took part in the bridal procession and remained for the wedding-feast (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧MARRIAGE⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧). In the above passages "the sons of the bride-chamber" are the disciples of Christ.
