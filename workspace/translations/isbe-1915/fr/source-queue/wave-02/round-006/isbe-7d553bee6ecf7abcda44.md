# BAR (1)
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bar (prefix): Aramaic for the Hebrew ben, "son." Compare Aramaic sections of Ezra and Daniel. In the Old Testament the word is found three times in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 31:2⟧ and once in Syriac ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 2:12⟧ (Hier. translates "pure"). In the New Testament "Bar" is frequently employed as prefix to names of persons. Compare Barabbas; Bar-Jesus; Bar-Jonah; Barnabas; Barsabbas; Bartholomew; Bartimeus.
