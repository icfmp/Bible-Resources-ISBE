# CARMELITE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kar'-mel-it (karmeli; Karmelios, Karmelites): A native of the Judean Carmel. Those who are thus named are Nabal, the husband of Abigail (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 30:5⟧, etc.), and Hezro (the King James Version Hezrai), one of David's mighty men (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Sa 23:35⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 3:3⟧ Septuagint reads tes Abigaias tes Karmelias, "of Abigail the Carmelitess" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Sa 27:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Ch 3:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
