# BETHER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
be'-ther (bether): In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:So 2:17⟧ mention is made of "the mountains of Bether." It is doubtful if a proper name is intended. The Revised Version, margin has, "perhaps, the spice malobathron." A Bether is prominent in late Jewish history as the place where the Jews resisted Hadrian under Bar Cochba in 135 AD. Its identity with Bittir, 7 miles Southwest of Jerusalem, is attested by an inscription.
