# BETH-ARABAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
beth-ar'-a-ba (beth ha-`arabhah; Baitharaba, "place of the Arabah"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) One of the 6 cities of Judah "in the wilderness" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 15:61⟧), on the borders of Benjamin and Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 15:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:18:18⟧ Septuagint). "The wilderness of Judah" is the barren land West of the Dead Sea. Beth-arabah is not yet identified.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(2) One of the cities of Benjamin (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jos 18:22⟧). Septuagint (Codex Vaticanus) reads Baithabara, and this may be correct. The names are early confounded.
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->
