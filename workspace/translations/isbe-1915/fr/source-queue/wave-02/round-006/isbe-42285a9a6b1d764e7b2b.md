# CASTAWAY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kast'-a-wa (adokimos, from dokimazo, "I test," "I approve after testing," hence, approved after being tested): This word is rendered "castaway" only in the King James Version: "I myself should be a castaway" ("rejected" the Revised Version (British and American), the American Standard Revised Version, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Co 9:27⟧). But the same word occurs a number of times usually translated "reprobate" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ro 1:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Co 13:5-7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:2Ti 3:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Tit 1:16⟧); "rejected" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Hebrews 6:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧
