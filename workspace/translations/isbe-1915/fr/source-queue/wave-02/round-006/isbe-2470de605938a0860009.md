# BAALATH-BEER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ba'-a-lath-be'-er ba`alath be'er "lady (mistress) of the well"; (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:8⟧ (in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 4:33⟧, Baal)): In Jos this place is designated "Ramah of the South," i.e. of the Negeb, while in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Sa 30:27⟧ it is described as Ramoth of the Negeb. It must have been a prominent hill (ramah = "height") in the far south of the Negeb and near a well be'er. The site is unknown though Conder suggests that the shrine Kubbet el Baul may retain the old name.
