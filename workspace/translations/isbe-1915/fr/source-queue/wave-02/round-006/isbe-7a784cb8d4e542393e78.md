# BEN-ABINADAB
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ben-a-bin'-a-dab, ben-ab-i-na'-dab (ben 'abhinadhabh, "son of Abinadab"): One of the "captains" of Solomon who provided for the king and his household, each for a month in the year (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 4:11⟧). His district was the region of Dor. In the King James Version he is called "the son of Abinadab." His wife was Tappath, the daughter of Solomon.
