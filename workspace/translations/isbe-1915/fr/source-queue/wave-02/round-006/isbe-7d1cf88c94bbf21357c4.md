# BAVVAI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
bav'-a-i (bawway; Septuagint Codex Alexandrinus, Benei; Codex Vaticanus, Bedei; the King James Version Bavai, "wisher" (?) (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ne 3:18⟧)): Perhaps identical with or a brother of Binnui (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ne 3:24⟧). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧BINNUI⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧. Bavvai, "the son of Henadad, the ruler of half the district of Keilah," was of a Levitical family. He is mentioned as one of those who repaired the wall of Jerusalem after the return from Babylon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ne 3:17⟧ f).
