# CABIN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kab'-in (chanuyyoth, "vaults"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 37:16⟧ the Revised Version (British and American), "cells"): In the East the prison often consisted of a pit (compare "dungeon-house" the Revised Version (British and American) and "house of the pit" the Revised Version, margin) with vaulted cells around it for the confinement of prisoners. The word is probably a gloss. The phrase "and into the cells" seems superfluous after "into the dungeonhouse."
