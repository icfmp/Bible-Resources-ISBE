# CHECKER-WORK; (NETWORK)
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
chek'-er-wurk (sebhakhah): This was a kind of ornamentation used on the tops of the pillars of Jachin and Boaz before the porch of the Temple (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 7:17⟧). Its exact form is not known. See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧TEMPLE⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧. For "a broidered coat" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ex 28:4⟧ the King James Version), the Revised Version (British and American) gives "a coat of checker work."
