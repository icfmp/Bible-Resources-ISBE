# CHANCELLOR
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
chan'-sel-er: The rendering in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 4:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:17⟧ of the Hebrew be`el Te`em; Septuagint Baal (4:9), Balgam (4:17), the latter being an incorrect translation of Hebrew `ayin. In 1 Esdras 2:16,25, Beeltethmos (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ezr 4:8⟧) occurs as a corruption, doubtless of be`el Te`em. The term in question designates an Assyrian office, namely, that of the "master or lord of official intelligence," or "postmaster" (Sayce).
