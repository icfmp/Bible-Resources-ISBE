# BRIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
qatseh or qetseh = "an extremity" (in a variety of applications and idioms), "border," "edge," "side," "shore" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 3:15⟧ the King James Version; the Revised Version (British and American) "brink"); also saphah or sepheth = "edge," "lip" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 7:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:26⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:2Ch 4:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:5⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Joh 2:7⟧, the adverb ano, is used to emphasize the verb egemisan = "to fill," thus giving the idea of "filling to the top."
