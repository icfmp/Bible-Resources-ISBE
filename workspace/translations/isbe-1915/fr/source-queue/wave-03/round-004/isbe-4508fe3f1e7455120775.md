# COMPASS; COMPASSES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kum'-pas, kum'-pas-iz: "Compass," noun, is the translation of chugh, "a circle," "vault" or "arch" ("when he set a compass upon the face of the depth" ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 8:27⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American), the American Standard Revised Version "circle"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 26:10⟧; and see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧CIRCLE⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧VAULT OF EARTH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧); of karkobh, "a margin" "border" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ex 27:5⟧, "the compass of the altar," the Revised Version (British and American) "the ledge round," so 38:4); the phrase "to fetch a compass" is the translation of sabhabh, "to turn about," "go round about" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Nu 34:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jos 15:3⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "turn about," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Sa 5:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:2Ki 3:9⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "make a circuit"); of perierchomai, "to go about" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ac 28:13⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "made a circuit"; margin "Some ancient authorities read cast loose"; see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:13⟧CIRCUIT⟦ISBE:CLOSE:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:14 -->
"Compasses" is the Revised Version (British and American) for "compass," mechughah, an instrument for describing a circle: "He marketh it out with the compasses" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Isa 44:13⟧) in making an idol.
<!-- ISBE-END:14 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:16 -->
The verb "to compass" occurs frequently in the senses of "to surround" and "to go round about," e.g. ⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ge 2:11⟧, "which compasseth the whole land of Havilah," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:De 2:1⟧, "We compassed (went around) mount Seir many days"; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Jer 31:22⟧ we have "A new thing on the earth: a woman shall compass a man," the Revised Version (British and American) "encompass"; possibly as a suitor; but more probably as a protector. In those happy days, the protection of women (under God, 31:28) will be sufficient, while the men are at their work; "to encompass" ("The cords of death compassed me" ⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Ps 18:4⟧; "the waves of death," ⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:2Sa 22:5⟧). "To gird" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Isa 50:11⟧ the Revised Version (British and American)); "to lie around," "to be laid around" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Heb 5:2⟧, "compassed with infirmity" (clothed with it); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Heb 12:1⟧, "compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses").
<!-- ISBE-END:16 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:25 -->
In Apocrypha we have "compassed about with yawning darkness" (The Wisdom of Solomon 19:17); "compassed the circuit of heaven" (Ecclesiasticus 24:5); "compassed with pomegranates of gold" (Ecclesiasticus 45:9); "The rainbow compasseth the heaven" (Ecclesiasticus 43:12); the course of the sun (1 Esdras 4:34).
