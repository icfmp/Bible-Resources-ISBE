# CONFIDENCE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon'-fi-dens (baTach, and forms, kecel; parrhesia; peitho, pepoithesis, hupostasis): The chief Hebrew word translated "confidence" (baTach, and its forms) means, perhaps, radically, "to be open," showing thus what originated the idea of "confidence"; where there was nothing hidden a person felt safe; it is very frequently rendered "trust." In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 118:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:9⟧ we have "It is better to take refuge in Yahweh than to put confidence in princes," and in 65:5, "O God of our salvation, thou that art the confidence (mibhTach) of all the ends of the earth." MibhTach is translated "confidence" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Job 18:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:31:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Pr 21:22⟧, etc.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
Kecel ("firmness," "stoutness") is rendered "confidence" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Pr 3:26⟧, and kiclah in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Job 4:6⟧; peitho ("to persuade") is translated "confidence" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:2Co 2:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ga 5:10⟧, etc.; pepoithesis, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:2Co 1:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:8:22⟧, etc.; hupostasis ("what stands under"), in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:2Co 11:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Heb 3:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:2Co 9:4⟧; parrhesia ("out- spokenness," "boldness") is invariably translated in the Revised Version (British and American) "boldness" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Ac 28:31⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Heb 3:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:4:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:20:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:10:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:21:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:1Jo 2:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:3:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:23:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:5:14⟧); tharseo or tharrheo ("to have good courage") is so translated in the Revised Version (British and American), "being therefore always of good courage" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:2Co 5:6⟧); "I am of good courage concerning you" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:2Co 7:16⟧), the King James Version "confident" and "confidence." Revised Version has "confidence" for "hope" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:27:Job 8:14⟧); for "assurance" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:Isa 32:17⟧); for "trust" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:2Co 3:4⟧); for "same confident boasting" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:30:2Co 9:4⟧); "is confident" for "trusted" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:31:Job 40:23⟧); "to have confidence" for "thinketh that he hath whereof he might trust" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:32:Php 3:4⟧); "confidently" for "constantly" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:33:Ac 12:15⟧); "confidently affirm" for "affirm" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:34:1Ti 1:7⟧); conversely, we have for "his confidence" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:35:Job 18:14⟧), "wherein he trusteth," for "with confidence" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:36:Eze 28:26⟧) "securely therein."
