# CONSUME
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sum' (akhal, kalah, tamam; analisko): In Old Testament 'akhal ("to eat," "devour") occurs very frequently, and is translated "consumed" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 31:40⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ex 15:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ps 78:63⟧, etc.); kalah ("to finish") is also frequently translated "consume," "consumed" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ge 41:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ex 32:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ps 59:13⟧, etc.); tamam, "to be perfect," "finished" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Nu 17:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:De 2:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ps 73:19⟧, etc.). There are many other words translated "consume" and "consumed," e.g. cuph, "to end" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Jer 8:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Zec 1:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:3⟧); balah, "to fade," "wear away" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Job 13:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ps 49:14⟧); gazal, implying violence (⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Job 24:19⟧); caphah, "to end" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Ge 19:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Isa 7:20⟧, etc.); ashesh, "to be old" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Ps 6:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:21:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:31:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:10⟧ the King James Version); maqaq, "to become completed" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Eze 4:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:24:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:Zec 14:12⟧ bis); `asah kalah is rendered "utterly consume" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Ne 9:31⟧); analisko, "to use up," occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:27:Lu 9:54⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:27:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:Ga 5:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:28:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:2Th 2:8⟧ (the King James Version); dapanao, "to spend," is translated "consume" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:30:Jas 4:3⟧ (the Revised Version (British and American) "spend"); katanalisko, "to consume utterly," occurs only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:31:Heb 12:29⟧; "for our God is a consuming fire."

In the Revised Version (British and American) "devour," "devoured" are several times substituted for "consume," "consumed," e.g. ⟦ISBE:KEEP:33:Job 20:26⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:33:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:34:Jer 49:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:34:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:35:Nu 16:35⟧; "boil well" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:36:Eze 24:10⟧); for "be consumed with dying" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:37:Nu 17:13⟧), "perish all of us"; "consume" is substituted for "corrupt" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:38:Mt 6:19⟧; "my spirit is consumed," for "my breath is corrupt" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:39:Job 17:1⟧); instead of "the flame consumeth the chaff" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:40:Isa 5:24⟧) we have "as the dry grass sinketh down in the flame"; and for "whom the Lord shall consume" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:41:2Th 2:8⟧), the Revised Version (British and American) reads (after a different text) "whom the Lord Jesus shall slay," "consume" in the American Revised Version, margin.
