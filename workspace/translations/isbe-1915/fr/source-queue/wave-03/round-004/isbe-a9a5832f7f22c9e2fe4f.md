# DIADEM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
di'-a-dem: There are seven Bible references to the diadem, four in the Old Testament and three in the New Testament. The Hebrew words do not mark any clear distinctions.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) tsaniph, tsanoph, tsaniphah (all from tsanaph, primarily "to wrap," "dress," "roll") mean a headdress in the nature of a turban or piece of cloth wrapped or twisted about the head. The word is also rendered "hood," "mitre." ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 29:14⟧: "My justice was as a robe and a diadem" (RVm, "turban"); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 62:3⟧: "a royal diadem in the hand of thy God."
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) tsephirah, means "a crown," "diadem," i.e. something round about the head; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 28:5⟧ "a diadem of beauty, unto the residue of his people."
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
(3) mitsnepheth, means an official turban or tiara of priest or king, translated also "mitre." ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Eze 21:26⟧: "Remove the mitre, and take off the crown."
<!-- ISBE-END:8 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(4) diadema, the Greek word in the New Testament for "diadem," means "something bound about the head." Found 3 t, all in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Re 12:3⟧: "a great red dragon .... and upon his heads seven diadems" (the King James Version "crowns"); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Re 13:1⟧: "a beast .... and on his horns ten diadems"; 19:11,12: "a white horse .... and upon his head are many diadems." See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:13⟧CROWN⟦ISBE:CLOSE:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧
