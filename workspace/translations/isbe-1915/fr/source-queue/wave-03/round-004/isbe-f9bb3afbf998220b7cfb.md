# DELIGHT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-lit' (verb, chaphets, ratsah, sha`a`; sunedomai): "To delight" is most frequently expressed by chaphets, which means originally "to bend" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Job 40:17⟧, "He moveth his tail"), hence, "to incline to," "take pleasure in." It is used of God's pleasure in His people (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 14:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 22:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ps 18:19⟧, etc.), and in righteousness, etc. (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 66:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jer 9:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Mic 7:18⟧, etc.), also of man's delight in God and His will (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ps 40:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:73:25⟧; the King James Version and the Revised Version (British and American), "There is none upon earth that I desire besides thee"), and in other objects (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ge 34:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:1Sa 18:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Es 2:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Isa 66:3⟧); sha`a`, "to stroke," "caress," "be fond of," occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ps 94:19⟧, "Thy comforts delight my soul"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ps 119:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:17:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:47⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:70⟧, "I will delight myself in thy statutes." Similarly, Paul says (⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Ro 7:22⟧), "I delight (sunedomai) in (margin, the Revised Version (British and American) "Greek with") the law of God after the inward man." This is the only occurrence of the word in the New Testament.
