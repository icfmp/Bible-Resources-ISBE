# DEED
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ded: Used in its ordinary modern sense in EV. In the Old Testament it is used to translates five Hebrew words: gemylah, literally, "recompense" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 59:18⟧); dabhar, literally, "word," "thing" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ch 35:27⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "acts"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Es 1:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jer 5:28⟧); ma`aseh (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ge 20:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:44:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ezr 9:13⟧); `alilah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Ch 16:8⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "doings"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ps 105:1⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "doings"); po`al (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ps 28:4⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "work"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Jer 25:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:15 -->
In the New Testament "deed" very frequently translates ergon (same root as English "work"; compare "energy"), which is still more frequently (espescially in the Revised Version (British and American)) rendered "work." In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Lu 23:51⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ac 19:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:17:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Ro 8:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Col 3:9⟧ the King James Version, the Revised Version (British and American) "doings," it stands for Greek praxis (literally, "a doing," "transaction"), each time in a bad sense, equivalent to wicked deed, crime, a meaning which is frequently associated with the plural of praxis (compare English "practices" in the sense of trickery; so often in Polybius; Deissmann maintains that praxis was a technical term in magic), although in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Mt 16:27⟧ (the King James Version "works") and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Ro 12:4⟧ the same Greek word has a neutral meaning. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Jas 1:25⟧ the King James Version "deed" is the translation of Greek poiesis, more correctly rendered "doing" in the Revised Version (British and American).
