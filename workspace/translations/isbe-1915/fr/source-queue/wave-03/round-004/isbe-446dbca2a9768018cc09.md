# COUNTRY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kun'-tri ('erets, "land," sadheh, "field"; agros, "field," chora, "region"): The foregoing are the principal words rendered "country" in English Versions of the Bible, though we find also 'adhamah, "earth" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jon 4:2⟧); 'i, "island" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jer 47:4⟧ the King James Version); gelilah, "circuit" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eze 47:8⟧ the King James Version); chebhel, "rope" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:De 3:14⟧); maqom, "place" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ge 29:26⟧ the King James Version); nepheth,"hill" or "height" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jos 17:11⟧ the King James Version); genos, "race" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ac 4:36⟧ the King James Version); ge, "earth" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Mt 9:31⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ac 7:3⟧ the King James Version); patris, "native land" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Lu 4:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Joh 4:44⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Heb 11:14⟧); perichoros, "country (the American Standard Revised Version "region") round about" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Mt 14:35⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Lu 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:4:37⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:17:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:8:37⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Heb 11:14⟧ ff, "heaven" is referred to as a country. Egypt and Assyria were "far countries" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Jer 8:19⟧ the King James Version; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Zec 10:9⟧). The hill country (compare the numerous Gibeahs (gibh`ah, "a hill")) was the mountainous region to the North or to the South of Jerusalem. The low country, shephelah (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:22⟧SHEPHELAH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:22⟧), consisted of the foothills to the west of the hill country. The south country or ⟦ISBE:OPEN:23⟧NEGEB⟦ISBE:CLOSE:23⟧ (neghebh), which see, was the dry, extreme southern part of Palestine, approximately between Beersheba and Kadesh-barnea.
