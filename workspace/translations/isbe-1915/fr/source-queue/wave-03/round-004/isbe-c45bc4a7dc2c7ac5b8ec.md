# DAY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
da (yom; hemera): This common word has caused some trouble to plain readers, because they have not noticed that the word is used in several different senses in the English Bible. When the different uses of the word are understood the difficulty of interpretation vanishes. We note several different uses of the word:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) It sometimes means the time from daylight till dark. This popular meaning is easily discovered by the context, e. g. ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ge 1:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:8:22⟧, etc. The marked periods of this daytime were morning, noon and night, as with us. See ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ps 55:17⟧. The early hours were sometimes called "the cool of the day" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ge 3:8⟧). After the exile the day. or daytime was divided into twelve hours and the night into twelve (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Mt 20:1-12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Joh 11:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ac 23:23⟧); 6 a. m. would correspond to the first hour, 9 a. m. to the third; 12 noon to the sixth, etc. The hours were longer during the longer days and shorter during the shorter days, since they always counted 12 hours between sunrise and sunset.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:11 -->
(2) Day also means a period of 24 hours, or the time from sunset to sunset. In Bible usage the day begins with sunset (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Le 23:32⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ex 12:15-20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:2Co 11:25⟧, where night is put before day). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:15⟧DAY AND NIGHT⟦ISBE:CLOSE:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:11 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:16 -->
(3) The word "day" is also used of an indefinite period, e. g "the day" or "day that" means in general "that time" (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ge 2:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:17:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Le 14:2⟧); "day of trouble" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Ps 20:1⟧); "day of his wrath" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:Job 20:28⟧); "day of Yahweh" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:21:Isa 2:12⟧); "day of the Lord" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:1Co 5:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:1Th 5:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:23:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:2Pe 3:10⟧); "day of salvation" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:2Co 6:2⟧);. "day of Jesus Christ" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Php 1:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:26:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:16 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:27 -->
(4) It is used figuratively also in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:Joh 9:4⟧, where "while it is day" means "while I have opportunity to work, as daytime is the time for work." In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:1Th 5:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:29:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:30:8⟧, "sons of the day" means spiritually enlightened ones.
<!-- ISBE-END:27 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:31 -->
(5) We must also bear in mind that with God time is not reckoned as with us (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:32:Ps 90:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:32:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:33:2Pe 3:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:33:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:31 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:34 -->
(6) The apocalyptic use of the word "day" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:35:Da 12:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:35:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:36:Re 2:10⟧, etc., is difficult to define. It evidently does not mean a natural day. See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:37⟧APOCALYPSE⟦ISBE:CLOSE:37⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:37:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:34 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:38 -->
(7) On the meaning of "day" in the story of Creation we note (a) the word "day" is used of the whole period of creation (⟦ISBE:KEEP:39:Ge 2:4⟧); (b) these days are days of God, with whom one day is as a thousand years; the whole age or period of salvation is called "the day of salvation"; see above. So we believe that in harmony with Bible usage we may understand the creative days as creative periods. See also ⟦ISBE:OPEN:40⟧ASTRONOMY⟦ISBE:CLOSE:40⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:40:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:41⟧CREATION⟦ISBE:CLOSE:41⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:41:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:42⟧EVOLUTION⟦ISBE:CLOSE:42⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:42:tail⟧

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:44 -->
Figurative: The word "day" is used figuratively in many senses, some of which are here given.
<!-- ISBE-END:44 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:45 -->
(1) The span of human life.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:46:Ge 5:4⟧: "And the days of Adam .... were eight hundred years." "And if thou wilt walk .... then I will lengthen thy days" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:47:1Ki 3:14⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:48:Ps 90:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:48:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:49:Isa 38:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:49:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:45 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:50 -->
(2) An indefinite time.--Existence in general: ⟦ISBE:KEEP:51:Ge 3:14⟧: "All the days of thy life" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:52:Ge 21:34⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:52:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:53:Nu 9:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:53:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:54:Jos 22:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:54:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:55:Lu 1:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:55:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:56:Ac 21:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:56:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:50 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:57 -->
(3) A set time.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:58:Ge 25:24⟧: "And when her days .... were fulfilled"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:59:Da 12:13⟧: "Thou shalt stand in thy lot, at the end of the days" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:60:Le 12:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:60:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:61:Da 2:44⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:61:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:57 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:62 -->
(4) A historic period.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:63:Ge 6:4⟧: "The Nephilim were in the earth in those days"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:64:Jud 17:6⟧: "In those days there was no king in Israel" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:65:1Sa 3:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:65:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:66:1Ch 5:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:66:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:67:Ho 2:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:67:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:62 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:68 -->
(5) Past time.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:69:Ps 18:18⟧: "the day of my calamity"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:70:Ps 77:5⟧: "I have considered the days of old" (of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:71:Mic 7:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:71:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:72:Mal 3:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:72:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:73:Mt 23:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:73:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:68 -->

(6) Future time.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:75:De 31:14⟧: "Thy days approach that thou must die"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:76:Ps 72:7⟧: "In his days shall ...." (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:77:Eze 22:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:77:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:78:Joe 2:29⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:78:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:79:Mt 24:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:79:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:80:2Pe 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:80:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:81:Re 9:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:81:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:74 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:82 -->
(7) The eternal.--In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:83:Da 7:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:83:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:84:13⟧, where God is called "the ancient of days."
<!-- ISBE-END:82 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:85 -->
(8) A season of opportunity.--⟦ISBE:KEEP:86:Joh 9:4⟧: "We must work the works of him that sent me, while it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:87:Ro 13:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:87:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:88:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:88:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:89:1Th 5:5-8⟧). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:90⟧DAY⟦ISBE:CLOSE:90⟧ (4), above.
