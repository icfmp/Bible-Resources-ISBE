# COL-HOZEH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kol-ho'-ze (kol-chozeh, "all seeing"; Septuagint omits): A man whose son Shallum rebuilt the fountain gate of Jerusalem in the days of Nehemiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ne 3:15⟧). The Col-hozeh of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ne 11:5⟧ is probably another man.
