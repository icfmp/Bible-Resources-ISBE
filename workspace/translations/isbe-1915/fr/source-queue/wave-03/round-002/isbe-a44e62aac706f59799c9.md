# DABBESHETH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dab'-e-sheth (dabbesheth; Dabasthai; the King James Version Dabbasheth, dab'a-sheth): A town on the western boundary of Zebulun (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:11⟧). It is probably identical with the modern Dabsheh, a ruined site to the East of Acre.
