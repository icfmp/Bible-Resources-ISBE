# DANNAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dan'-a (dannah): One of the cities in the hill country of Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:49⟧) between Socoh and Kiriath-sannah (Debir), probably Idhna--the Iedna of the Onom--8 miles W. of Hebron. See PEF, III, 305, 330.
