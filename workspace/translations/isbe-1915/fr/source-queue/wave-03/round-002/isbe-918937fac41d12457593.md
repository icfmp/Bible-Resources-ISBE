# CHOENIX
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke'-niks (choinix): A Greek dry measure, almost equal to one quart. Mentioned in the New Testament only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Re 6:6⟧, where the Revised Version, margin would read "choenix" instead of the indefinite translation "measure." The verse is then obviously a threat of famine.
