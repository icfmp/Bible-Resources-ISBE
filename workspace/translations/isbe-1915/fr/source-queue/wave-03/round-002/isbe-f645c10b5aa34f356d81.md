# DEHAITES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-ha'-tez (dehawe'; the King James Version Dehavites): A people enumerated in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 4:9⟧ with Elamites, ere, as among those settled by the Assyrian king Osnappar (Assurbanipal) in Samaria. The identification is uncertain.
