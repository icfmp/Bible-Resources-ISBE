# DEHORT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dē-hôrt' (ἀποστρέφω, apostréphō; RV DISSUADE): Not found in the Eng. Bible; once only in Apoc (1 Macc 9 9). An obsolete Eng. word; the opposite of "exhort." It means "to dissuade," "to forbid," "to restrain from."
