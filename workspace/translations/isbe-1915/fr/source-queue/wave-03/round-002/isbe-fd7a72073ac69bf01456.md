# DEMOPHON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dem'-o-fon (Demophon): A Syrian general in Palestine under Antiochus V (Eupator) who continued to harass the Jews after covenants had been made between Lysias and Judas Maccabeus (2 Macc 12:2).
