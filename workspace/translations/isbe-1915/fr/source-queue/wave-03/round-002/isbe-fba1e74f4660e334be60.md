# DILEAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dil'-e-an (dil`an, "cucumber"): A town in the Shephelah of Judah named with Migdal-gad and Mizpeh (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:38⟧, the English Revised Version "Dilan"), which lay probably on the North of Lachish and Eglon. It has not been identified.
