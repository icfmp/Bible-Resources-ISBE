# CRESCENS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kres'-enz (Kreskes, "increasing"): An assistant of Paul, mentioned in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ti 4:10⟧ as having gone to Galatia. That he was one of the Seventy, and that he founded the church in Vienna in Gaul, are traditions without any trustworthy basis.
