# COCK-CROWING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kok'-kro-ing (alektorophonia): An indefinite hour of the night between midnight and morning (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mr 13:35⟧), referred to by all the evangelists in their account of Peter's denial (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mt 26:34⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:74⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Mr 14:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Lu 22:34⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Joh 13:38⟧). It is derived from the habit of the cock to crow especially toward morning.
