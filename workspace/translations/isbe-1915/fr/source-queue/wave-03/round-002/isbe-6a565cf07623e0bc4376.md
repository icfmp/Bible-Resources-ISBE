# CHESIL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke'-sil, kes'-il (kecil; A, Chaseir): A town in the extreme South of Judah named with Eltolad, Hormah and Ziklag (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:30⟧). The name does not occur again. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 19:4⟧ it is replaced by Bethul (Septuagint Baithel), and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ch 4:30⟧ by Bethuel. "Chesil" may have arisen from a misreading of the text.
