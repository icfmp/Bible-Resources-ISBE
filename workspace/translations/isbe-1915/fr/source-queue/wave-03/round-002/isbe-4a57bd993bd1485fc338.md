# EAST COUNTRY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kun'-tri ('erets mizrach):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
Lit. "country of the sunrise" over against the "country of the sunset" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Zec 8:7⟧). The two together form a poetical expression indicating the whole earth.
