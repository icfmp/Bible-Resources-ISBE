# CHESULLOTH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke-sul'-oth (ha-keculloth; B, Chasaloth, A, Achesaloth): A town on the border of Zebulun (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:18⟧), the same as Chisloth-tabor (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 19:12⟧). It is represented by the modern village Iksal on the northern edge of Esdraelon, circa 3 miles West of Mt. Tabor.
