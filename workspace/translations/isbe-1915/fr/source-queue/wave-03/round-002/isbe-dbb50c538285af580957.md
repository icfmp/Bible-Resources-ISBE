# CROSSWAY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kros'-wa (perek, literally, "division"): A forking or dividing of the way. Obadiah warns Edom, "And stand thou not in the crossway, to cut off those of his that escape" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Obad 1:14⟧). In Septuagint, "a mountain pass."
