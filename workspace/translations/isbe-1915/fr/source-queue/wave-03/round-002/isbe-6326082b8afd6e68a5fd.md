# CORDS, SMALL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kordz (schoinion, the diminutive of schoinos, "a rush," hence, "a rope of rushes"): Translated "small cords" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Joh 2:15⟧ the King James Version; the Revised Version (British and American) "cords"). The same word is translated "ropes" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ac 27:32⟧. See also ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Job 41:2⟧ margin.
