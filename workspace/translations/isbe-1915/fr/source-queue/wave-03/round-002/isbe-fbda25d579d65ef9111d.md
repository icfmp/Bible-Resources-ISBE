# DALAN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
da'-lan (Dalan; the King James Version Ladan): Head of a family that returned to Jerusalem, but which "could shew neither their families, nor their stock, how they were of Israel" (1 Esdras 5:37); corresponds to Delaiah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 2:60⟧). Another reading is "Asan."
