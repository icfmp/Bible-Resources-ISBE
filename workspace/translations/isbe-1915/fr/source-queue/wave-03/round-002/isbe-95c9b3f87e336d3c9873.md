# CUN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kun (kun, A, ek ton eklekton poleon, "from the chosen cities"): One of the cities of Hadarezer, king of Syria, spoiled by David (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ch 18:8⟧, the King James Version "Chun"). In the parallel passage (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Sa 8:8⟧) its place is taken by ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧BEROTHAH⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧, which see.
