# CRASHING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
krash'-ing (shebher): This word, meaning "a breach," figuratively "destruction," is translated "crashing" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ze 1:10⟧: "a great crashing from the hills," representing the doom to fall on evil-doers in Jerusalem, as the enemy advanced against the city from the north.
