# DAKUBI
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
da-ku'-bi, da-koo'-bi (Dakoub, Dakoubi; the King James Version Dacobi): Head of a family of gate-keepers (1 Esdras 5:28) called "Akkub" in the canonical lists.
