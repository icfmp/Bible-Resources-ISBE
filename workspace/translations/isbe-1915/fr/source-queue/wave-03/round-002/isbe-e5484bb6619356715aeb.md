# COMMODIOUS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ko-mo'-di-us (aneuthetos, "not well placed"): The word occurs only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ac 27:12⟧. "As regards wintering, the place was certainly `not commodious,' but as regards shelter from some winds (including Northwest), it was a good anchorage" (Code of Hammurabi, XXIII, 639).
