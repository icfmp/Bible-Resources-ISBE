# DAMSEL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dam'-zel: A young, unmarried woman; a girl (lass); maiden (compare French demoiselle). The Revised Version (British and American) in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 26:69⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Joh 18:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ac 12:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:16:16⟧ gives "maid" for paidiske, "a girl," i.e. (spec.) a maidservant or young female slave (the King James Version "damsel"), and "child" for paidion, "a half-grown boy or girl," in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Mr 5:39⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:40⟧ bis. 41.
