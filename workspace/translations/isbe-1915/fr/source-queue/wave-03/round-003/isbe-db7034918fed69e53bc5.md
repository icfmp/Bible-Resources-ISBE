# DRAUGHT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
draft (aphedron; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 15:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mr 7:19⟧): "Closet," "sink" or "privy" (Rheims), literally, "place for sitting apart" (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Ki 10:27⟧, "draught-house," and Mishna "water-house"). According to the Mishna, Jehu turned the temple of Baal in Samaria into public latrines, "waterhouses." Mark adds here (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Mr 7:19⟧) that by this saying Jesus cleansed all articles of food, i.e., declared them to be clean.
