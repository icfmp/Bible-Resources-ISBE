# CONSUMMATION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-su-ma'-shun (killayon from kalah): The word, meaning destruction, completion, or failing (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 10:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:28:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Da 9:27⟧) is translated interchangeably in the King James Version for another Hebrew word referring to a physical disease, and best translated "consumption"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Le 26:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:De 28:22⟧. Not used in the Revised Version (British and American). The Hebrew variously but more accurately translated "full end"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Da 9:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Isa 10:23⟧; and "destruction"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Isa 10:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:28:22⟧. There seems therefore to be an inconsistency on the part of both the Authorized and Revised translations.
