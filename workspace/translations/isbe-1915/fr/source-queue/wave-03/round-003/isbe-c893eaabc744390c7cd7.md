# DAM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
('em, ordinary Hebrew word for "mother"): Hebrew law prohibited the destruction of the "dam" and the young of birds at the same time, commanding that if the young be taken from a nest the dam be allowed to escape (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 22:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:7⟧). In the same spirit it enjoined the taking of an animal for slaughter before it had been seven days with its "dam" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ex 22:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Le 22:27⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ex 23:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
