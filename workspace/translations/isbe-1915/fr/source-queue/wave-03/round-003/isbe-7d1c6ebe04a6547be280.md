# DELECTABLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-lek'-ta-bl (chamadh, "to desire"): Found only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 44:9⟧, King James Version: "Their delectable things shall not profit," the King James Version margin"desirable." the American Standard Revised Version translates: "the things that they delight in." The reference is to idols or images. Delitzsch renders the phrase: "Their darlings are good for nothing." The word may be traced back to the Latin delectabilis, "pleasant," or "delightful."
