# DESTRUCTION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-struk'-shun: In the King James Version this word translates over 30 Hebrew words in the Old Testament, and 4 words in the New Testament. Of these the most interesting, as having a technical sense, is 'abhaddon (from verb 'abhadh, "to be lost," "to perish"). It is found 6 times in the Wisdom Literature, and nowhere else in the Old Testament; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Re 9:11⟧. See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧ABADDON⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧
