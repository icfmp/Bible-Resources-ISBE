# CLOD
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Job 7:5⟧ (gish, gush, "a mass of earth"), "clods of dust," the crust of his sores, formed by the dry, swollen skin--a symptom of leprosy, though not peculiar to it. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 21:33⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:38:38⟧ (reghebh, "a soft clod," "lump of clay"), "The clods of the valley shall be sweet unto him," "The clods cleave fast together." In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Joe 1:17⟧ (meghraphah, "a furrow," "something thrown off" (by the spade)), "The seeds rot (m "shrivel") under their clods."
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
Figurative: "Jacob shall break his clods" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ho 10:11⟧), i.e. "must harrow for himself," used figuratively of spiritual discipline (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Isa 28:24⟧ the King James Version).
