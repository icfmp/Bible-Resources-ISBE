# CHESALON
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kes'-a-lon (kecalon; Chaslon, Chasalon): One of the cities on the Northern boundary of Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 15:10⟧). In the 4th century it was a "very large village." It is now Kesla, 2,087 ft. above sea-level, a small village perched on a mountain ridge to the South of Wady el Humar. See Palestine Exploration Fund, III, 25, 26; Sh XVII.
