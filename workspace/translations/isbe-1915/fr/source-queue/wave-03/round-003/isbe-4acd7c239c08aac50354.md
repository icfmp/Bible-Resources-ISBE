# COUNSEL; COUNSELLOR
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
koun'-sel, koun'-seler (sumboulion): Ordinarily found as object of verb "to take" or "to give," expressing, beside the idea of a practical end to be reached, that of consultation and deliberation among those united in a common cause (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 12:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mr 3:6⟧). A counselor (sumboulos) is a confidential adviser (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ro 11:34⟧); often in the Old Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 9:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Pr 24:6⟧, etc.). Confounded in the King James Version with "councillor" (see above), the latter being an official adviser, which the former does not necessarily mean.
