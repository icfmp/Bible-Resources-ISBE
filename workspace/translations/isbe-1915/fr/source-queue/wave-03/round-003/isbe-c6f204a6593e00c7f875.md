# CIRCUIT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
sur'-kit, "a going around": Used to represent several Hebrew words in several senses, e.g. the sun's orbit (tequphah), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 19:6⟧; the vault of the heavens (chugh), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 22:14⟧ the King James Version; the circuit of the winds (cabhibh), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ec 1:6⟧ (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧ASTRONOMY⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧); Samuel's visiting of communities (cabhabh), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Sa 7:16⟧. In the Revised Version (British and American) the idea of encircling or "fetching a compass" (the King James Version) is expressed by the phrase "to make a circuit" (hacebh), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Sa 5:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Ki 3:9⟧; and in the Revised Version, margin it indicates a plain (ha-kikkar), ⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ne 3:22⟧. The Greek perielthontes is translated in the same way (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ac 28:13⟧), but the Revised Version, margin reads "cast loose," following the Westcott and Hort, The New Testament in Greek reading perielontes.
