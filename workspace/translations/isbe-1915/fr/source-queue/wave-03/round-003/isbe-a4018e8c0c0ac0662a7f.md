# COZ
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
koz (kots, "thorn"): A man of Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ch 4:8⟧). the American Standard Revised Version has added the article, making the name Hakkoz without sufficient reason. The name occurs with the article (Ha-qots) in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ezr 2:61⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ne 3:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:7:63⟧, and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Ch 24:10⟧, but not with reference to the same person. Coz was of the tribe of Judah, while Hakkoz belonged to the family of Aaron.
