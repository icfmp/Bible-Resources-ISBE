# CLOUT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
klout: As substantive (ha-cechabhoth) a patch or piece of cloth, leather, or the like, a rag, a shred, or fragment. Old "cast clouts and old rotten rags" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 38:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:12⟧ the King James Version). As verb (Tala') "to bandage," "patch," or mend with a clout. "Old shoes and clouted (the American Standard Revised Version "patched") upon their feet" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 9:5⟧); compare Shakespeare, Cym., IV, 2: "I thought he slept, And put my clouted brogues from off my feet"; Milton, Comus: "And the dull swain treads on it daily with his clouted shoon."
