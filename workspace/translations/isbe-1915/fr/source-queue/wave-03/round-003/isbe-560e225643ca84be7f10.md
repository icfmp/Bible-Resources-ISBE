# CLAW
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
klo (parcah, literally, "hoof"): One of the marks of a "clean" animal is stated thus: "Every beast that parteth the hoof, and cleaveth the cleft into two claws, ye shall eat" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 14:6⟧ the King James Version; the Revised Version (British and American) "hath the hoof cloven in two"). See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧CHEW⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧CUD⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧. the King James Version uses the word "claws" where the Revised Version (British and American) supplies "hoofs" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Zec 11:16⟧, "and will tear their hoofs in pieces," as the sheep are being overdriven. In the only other passage containing the word (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Da 4:33⟧) there is no Hebrew equivalent in the original--"his nails like birds' (claws)."
