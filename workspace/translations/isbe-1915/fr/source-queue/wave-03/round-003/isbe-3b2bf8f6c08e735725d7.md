# CLEAR; CLEARNESS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kler, kler'-nes (bar; diablepo): Equivalent of several Hebrew and Greek words for bright, unclouded, shining without obstruction, distinct, brilliant; "clearer than the noon-day" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Job 11:17⟧): "clear as the sun" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:So 6:10⟧); "clear shining after rain" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 23:4⟧); "clear heat in sunshine" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 18:4⟧); "clear as crystal" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Re 21:11⟧). Adverb, "clearly," for distinctly (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Mt 7:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Mr 8:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ro 1:20⟧). Noun, "clearness," for brilliancy, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ex 24:10⟧, "as the very heaven for clearness."
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:12 -->
From this physical, it is applied, in a moral sense, to character, as spotless and free from guilt, or charge, or obligation "from oath" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Ge 24:8⟧); "from transgression" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Ps 19:13⟧). Hence, the verb "to clear" means juridically to declare or prove innocent, to vindicate (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Ge 44:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ex 34:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Nu 14:18⟧; compare hagnos, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:2Co 7:11⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "pure") "Be clear when thou judgest" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Ps 51:4⟧) refers to the proof and vindication of the righteousness of God.
