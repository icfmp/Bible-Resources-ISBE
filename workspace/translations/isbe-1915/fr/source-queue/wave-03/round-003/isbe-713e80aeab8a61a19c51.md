# CURE; CURES
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kur: Represents the words gahah, marpe', raphdh; therapeuo, iasis. Gahah in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 17:22⟧ translated "medicine" means properly the removal of a bandage from a healed wound, and, is used figuratively in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ho 5:13⟧; marpe', "healing," is used in the sense of deliverance of the city in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jer 33:6⟧; with a negative particle in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:2Ch 21:18⟧ it is used to describe the bowel disease of Jehoram as incurable. The Greek words are used of physical cures (iasis in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Lu 13:32⟧) as contradistinguished from the casting out of demons as ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Mt 17:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Lu 7:21⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Joh 5:10⟧. Cure is only used in the New Testament in the sense of physical healing; in the Old Testament usually in the sense of spiritual or national deliverance from danger.
