# CONFEDERATE; CONFEDERACY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-fed'-er-at, kon-fed'-er-a-si: "Confederate" as an adjective in the sense of united or leagued is twice the translation of berith "covenant," in several instances translated "league" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 14:13⟧, ba`al berith, "lord or master of a covenant," "an ally,") "these were confederate with Abram"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 83:5⟧; once of nuach, "to rest," "Syria is confederate with Ephraim" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 7:2⟧, the Revised Version, margin "resteth on Ephraim"; also 1 Macc 10:47).
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
As a noun "confederate" occurs in 1 Macc 10:16, summachos, "confederates" (1 Macc 8:20,24,31; 14:40; 15:17).
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
Confederacy, as a "league," occurs as the translation of berith, "the men of thy confederacy" (Obad 1:7); as a conspiracy it occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 8:12⟧ twice, as translation of qesher from qashar, "to bind": "Say ye not, a confederate." Compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Sa 15:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Ki 12:20⟧, etc.
