# DEVOTION; DEVOTIONS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-vo'-shun, (sebasmata): For the King James Version "your devotions" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ac 17:23⟧), the Revised Version (British and American) has "the objects of your worship," which is probably the intended meaning of the King James Version. the Revised Version (British and American) reads "devotion" for the King James Version "prayer" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 15:4⟧ (the Revised Version, margin "meditation," Hebrew siach).
