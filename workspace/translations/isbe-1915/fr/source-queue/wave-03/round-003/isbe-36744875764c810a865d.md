# CHERUB
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke'-rub (kerubh, Cheroub, Charoub): A place in Babylonia from which people whose genealogies had fallen into confusion went up at the return from exile (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ezr 2:59⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ne 7:61⟧); unidentified. In 1 Esdras 5:36 we read "Charaathalan leading them, and Allar," a phrase that seems to have arisen through confusion of the names in the passages cited above.
