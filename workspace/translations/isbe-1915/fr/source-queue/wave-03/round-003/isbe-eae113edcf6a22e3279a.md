# CRISPING PINS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kris'-ping: Pins for crisping, or curling, the hair. Thus the King James Version renders Hebrew chariTim (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 3:22⟧; compare Vulgate). the Revised Version (British and American) substitutes more correctly "satchels" (so Qimchi (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ki 5:23⟧); compare Arabic). Others think of girdles; still others of veils or head-bands.
