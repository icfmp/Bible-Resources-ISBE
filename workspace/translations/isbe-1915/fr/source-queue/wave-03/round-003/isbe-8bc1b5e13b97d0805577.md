# CURDLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kur'-dl (qapha', "to congeal," "harden," "curdle"): Occurs in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Job 10:10⟧, "Hast thou not .... curdled me like cheese?" i.e. made him take solid form. "The formation of the embryo is a mystery on which the Hebrew dwells with a deep and reverential awe: compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 139:13-16⟧." These similes are often met with in the Koran and oriental poetry. See Speaker's Commentary in the place cited
