# CONTRADICTION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-tra-dik'-shun: the King James Version for antilogia (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Heb 7:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:12:3⟧). In the former passage, the Revised Version (British and American) has "without any dispute," i.e. what has been said requires no argument; in the latter "gainsaying" which is scarcely an improvement, the reference being to the oral attacks upon the words and character of Jesus.
