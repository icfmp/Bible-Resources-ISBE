# DAILY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
da'-li: This word, coming as it does from the Hebrew yom "day," and the Greek hemera, suggests either day by day (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ex 5:13⟧), that which is prepared for one daily (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ne 5:18⟧), as e.g. our "daily bread," meaning bread sufficient for that day (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mt 6:11⟧); or day by day continuously, one day after another in succession, as "the daily burnt offering" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Nu 29:6⟧ the King James Version), "daily ministration" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ac 6:1⟧), and "daily in the temple" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ac 5:42⟧ the King James Version). The meaning of the word "daily" as used in the Lord's Prayer (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Mt 6:11⟧) seems to indicate sufficient for our need, whether we consider that need as a day at a time, or day after day as we are permitted to live. "Give us bread sufficient for our sustenance."
