# COUPLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kup'-l:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) Used as a noun, indicates two objects of the same kind that are considered together. Thus we read of a couple of cakes (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Sa 13:6⟧, used loosely), and a couple of asses (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 16:1⟧, Hebrew tsemedh).
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) Used as a verb, it means to join or fasten one thing to another. This term occurs most frequently in the description of the tabernacle (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ex 26:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:36:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:16⟧). Couple is used in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:1Pe 3:2⟧ to describe the joining of fear to chaste behavior (Hebrew chabhar) .
