# DILIGENCE; DILIGENT; DILIGENTLY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dil'-i-jens, dil'-i-jent-li: This word is used in various senses in our English Bibles.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
1. In the Old Testament:
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ezr 5:8⟧, "with diligence" means "with care"; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ezr 6:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:7:17⟧, "with speed," "speedily"; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Pr 4:23⟧ "watchfulness"; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:De 4:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:6:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:19:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ps 77:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Pr 27:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Isa 55:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Mic 7:3⟧, "with care," "scrupulously," "earnestly." Sometimes it means "early" "with haste" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Job 8:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Pr 8:17⟧). It may mean "industrious," "exacting" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:Pr 10:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:18:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:12:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:22:29⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:20:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:21 -->
2. In the New Testament:
<!-- ISBE-END:21 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:22 -->
The American revisers have rendered "diligence" for various words in the King James Version, e. g. for "business" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Ro 12:11⟧; "giving diligence" for "endeavoring" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:Eph 4:3⟧); "give diligence" for "study" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:2Ti 2:15⟧,), for "labor" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:Heb 4:11⟧); "diligently" for "carefully" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:27:Php 2:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:27:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:28:Heb 12:17⟧); "be diligent in" for "meditate upon" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:29:1Ti 4:15⟧). It is well also to remember that the Old English meaning of diligence is "with love," from diligo, "to love."
