# DECEIT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-set' (mirmah; (dolos)): The intentional misleading or beguiling of another; in Scripture represented as a companion of many other forms of wickedness, as cursing (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 10:7⟧), hatred (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Pr 26:24⟧), theft, covetousness, adultery, murder (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mr 7:22⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ro 1:29⟧). The Revised Version (British and American) introduces the word in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Pr 14:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Th 2:10⟧; but in such passages as ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ps 55:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Pr 20:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:26:26⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:1Th 2:3⟧, renders a variety of words, more accurately than the King James Version, by "oppression," "falsehood," "guile," "error."
