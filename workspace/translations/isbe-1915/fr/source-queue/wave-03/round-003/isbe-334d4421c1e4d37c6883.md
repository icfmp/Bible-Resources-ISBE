# CONCOURSE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon'-kors (hamah, "to hum," "to make a noise"; sustrophe, "a turning" or "twisting together"): Hamah, usually translated by some word signifying "sound" is rendered "concourse" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 1:21⟧ (perhaps from the noise made by people thronging and talking together; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ki 1:41⟧, "uproar"), "She (wisdom) crieth in the chief place of concourse," the Revised Version, margin, Hebrew "at the head of the noisy (streets)"; sustrophe is translated "concourse" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ac 19:40⟧), a riotous crowd. Compare Judith 10:18.
