# CUSHITE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kush'-it: Whereas kushi, is elsewhere rendered Ethiopian, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Sa 18:21-32⟧ it is rendered Cushite in the Revised Version (British and American) (see CUSHI and compare ⟦ISBE:OPEN:4⟧CUSHITE WOMAN⟦ISBE:CLOSE:4⟧). Its plural, which occurs in Zephaniah, Daniel and 2 Chronicles, also in the form kushiyim, in Amos, is uniformly translated Ethiopians, following Septuagint. The other Old Testament books use simply kush, for people as well as land.
