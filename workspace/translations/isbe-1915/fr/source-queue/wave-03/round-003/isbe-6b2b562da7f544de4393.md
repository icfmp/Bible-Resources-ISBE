# CONCERNING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-surn'-ing:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
The Revised Version (British and American) makes frequent changes, such as "for," "as for," "from," "about," for "concerning"; "concerning" instead of "for," "of," "over," "in," "against," etc. Some of the other changes are, "unto that which is good" for "concerning" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ro 16:19⟧), "concerning" instead of "because of" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jer 23:9⟧), for "the miracle of" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Mr 6:52⟧); for "with" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Mr 10:41⟧), for "of the Lord" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ac 18:25⟧), "concerning Jesus" (diferent text), "by way of disparagement" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Co 11:21⟧), instead of "concerning reproach"; "Why askest thou me concerning that which is good?" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Mt 19:17⟧) instead of "Why callest thou me good?" (different text; see the Revised Version, margin).
