# CORN
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
korn (daghan; sitos): A word used for cereals generally (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 27:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:37⟧, etc., the King James Version) much as our English word "corn." the American Standard Revised Version almost invariably substitutes "grain" for "corn." The latter may be taken to include
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
(1) barley,
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
(2) wheat,
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(3) fitches (vetches),
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
(4) lentils,
<!-- ISBE-END:8 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:9 -->
(5) beans,
<!-- ISBE-END:9 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(6) millet,
<!-- ISBE-END:10 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:11 -->
(7) rye--the wrong translation for vetches,
<!-- ISBE-END:11 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:12 -->
(8) pulse--for all these see separate articles.
