# CHEPHIRAH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke-fi'-ra (ha-kephirah; Codex Vaticanus, Kapheira (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 9⟧); Codex Alexandrinus has Chepheira, Codex Vaticanus has kai Pheira (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 18⟧)): One of the cities of the Hivites who by guile made alliance with Israel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 9:17⟧). It was in the lot of Benjamin (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Jos 18:26⟧), and was reoccupied after the return from Babylon (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ezr 2:25⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Ne 7:29⟧). It is represented by the modern Kefireh, to the Southwest of Gibeon, and North of Karyat el-`Anab. It stands on high ground, with many ancient remains.
