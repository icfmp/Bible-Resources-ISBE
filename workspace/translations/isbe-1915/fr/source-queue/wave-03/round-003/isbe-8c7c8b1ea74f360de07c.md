# DESCRIBE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-skrib': This verb, now obsolete, in the sense used in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 18:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:9⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jud 8:14⟧, is a translation of kathabh, usually rendered "to write" or "inscribe." But in the above passages it has the Old English meaning of dividing into parts or into lots, as for example: "Walk through the land, and describe it according to their inheritance" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jos 18:4⟧); that is, describe in writing the location and size of the several parcels of land thus portioned out. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jud 8:14⟧ "described" should be translated "wrote down a list of." "Describe" occurs twice in the King James Version of the New Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Ro 4:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:10:5⟧), where lego, and grapho, are both rendered "describeth." the Revised Version (British and American) corrects both, and substitutes "pronounceth" in the first and "writeth" in the second passage.
