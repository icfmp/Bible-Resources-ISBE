# CRISPUS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kris'-pus (Krispos, "curled"): One of the small number baptized by Paul among the Corinthian Christians (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Co 1:14⟧). He had been ruler of the Jewish synagogue, but he "believed in the Lord with all his house"; and, following Paul, withdrew from the synagogue (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ac 18:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:8⟧). He seems to have been succeeded by Sosthenes (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ac 18:17⟧). According to tradition he became bishop of Aegina.
