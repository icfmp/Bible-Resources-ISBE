# DABERATH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dab'-e-rath (ha-dabherath, "pasture"; Dabeiroth): A city in the territory of Issachar, on the boundary between that tribe and Zebulun (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jos 19:12⟧). It was assigned to the Gershonite Levites (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 21:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ch 6:72⟧). The most probable identification is with Dabuiriyeh, a village on the lower western slopes of Tabor.
