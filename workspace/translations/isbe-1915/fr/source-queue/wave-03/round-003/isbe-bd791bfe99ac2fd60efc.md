# COUCHING-PLACE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kouch'-ing-plas (marbets; once in English Versions of the Bible, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Eze 25:5⟧): The same Hebrew word, however, which means simply "place of lying down" of animals in repose, is used also in Ze 2:15 where the translation is "a place .... to lie down in." The figure, a common one in Scripture (see besides, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Isa 17:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:27:10⟧), suggests desolation.
