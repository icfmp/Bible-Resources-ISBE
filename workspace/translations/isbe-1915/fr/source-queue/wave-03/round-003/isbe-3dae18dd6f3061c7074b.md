<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:1 -->
# EARTH, VAULT, OF THE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
volt:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
In one passage God is said to have "founded his vault ('aghuddah) upon the earth" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Am 9:6⟧). It is not quite certain whether this dome or vault refers to the earth itself, or to the heavens arched above it.
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:5 -->
The latter is the usual interpretation, but in either case the reference is rather to the strength of the structure than to its form; the word implying something that is firmly bound together and hence, an arch or dome because of its stability.
<!-- ISBE-END:5 -->
