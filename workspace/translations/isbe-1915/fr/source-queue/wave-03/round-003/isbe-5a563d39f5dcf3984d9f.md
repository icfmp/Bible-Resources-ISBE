# DAY, LAST
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(he eschate hemera): Repeatedly used by Jesus in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Joh 6:39⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:40⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:44⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:54⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:11:24⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:12:48⟧, for the day of resurrection and judgment (see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:9⟧ESCHATOLOGY OF THE NEW TESTAMENT⟦ISBE:CLOSE:9⟧). Compare the usage in the Old Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Isa 2:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Mic 4:1⟧) and the New Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Ac 2:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:2Ti 3:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:2Pe 3:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:1 Joh 2:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Jude 1:18⟧) of "last days" and "last time" to denote the Messianic age.

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:21 -->
In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Joh 7:37⟧, "the last day, the great day of the feast" refers to the eighth day of the feast of Tabernacles. This closing day was observed as a Sabbath (⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Le 23:36⟧). On it the libation of water made on other days was not made; hence, the allusion of Jesus to Himself as the Giver of the living water.
