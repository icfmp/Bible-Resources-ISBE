# CRIB
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
('ebhuc): "Crib" translates the Hebrew word [~'ebhuc] exactly, as it denotes "a barred receptacle for fodder used in cowsheds and foldyards; also in fields, for beasts lying out in the winter." The Hebrew is from a word meaning to feed ('abhac), and is used in the precise sense of the English word in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Job 39:9⟧ of the "crib" of the wild ox, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Pr 14:4⟧, "Where no oxen are, the crib is clean," and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Isa 1:3⟧, "The ox knoweth his owner, and the ass his master's crib." section 23.
