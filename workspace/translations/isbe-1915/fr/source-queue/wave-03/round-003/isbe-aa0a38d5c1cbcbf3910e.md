# EBEN-EZER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
eb-en-e'-zer ('ebhen ha-`ezer, "stone of the help"; Abenezer):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) Here Israel was defeated by the Philistines, 4,000 men falling in the battle (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Sa 4:1⟧ ff). It appears also to have been the scene of the disaster when the ark of God was captured (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Sa 4:3⟧ ff). The place is not identified. It was over against Aphek; but this site is also unknown (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Jos 12:18⟧). Eusebius, Onomasticon places it between Jerusalem and Ascalon, in the neighborhood of Beth-shemesh. Conder suggests Deir Aban, fully 2 miles East of `Ain Shems (PEF, III, 24).
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
(2) A stone set up by Samuel to perpetuate the memory of the signal victory granted to Israel over the Philistines in answer to his prayer (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Sa 7:12⟧). It stood between Mizpeh and Shen. The latter is probably identical with `Ain Sinia, North of Bethel. This defines the district in which it may be found; but no identification is yet possible.
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->
