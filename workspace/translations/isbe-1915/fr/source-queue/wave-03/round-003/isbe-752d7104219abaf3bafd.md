# CONSIST
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sist' (sunistemi): To stand together, exist, subsist (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Col 1:17⟧, "in him all things consist," i.e. the continuance of the universe is dependent upon His support and administration). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 12:15⟧, it translates the verb eimi, "to be," to express the thought that wealth is only an accident, not an essential to the highest ideal of life.
