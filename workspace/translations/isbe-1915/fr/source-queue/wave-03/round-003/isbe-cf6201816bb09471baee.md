# CRUMB
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
krum (psichion, "a little bit"): Occurs only in the New Testament, of remnants of food, scraps. Lazarus desired "to be fed with the crumbs that fell from the rich man's table" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 16:21⟧). "Even the (little) dogs eat of the crumbs" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mt 15:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mr 7:28⟧), "possibly the fragments of bread on which the guests wiped their hands (after thrusting them into the common dish), and flung to the dogs" (Farrar, Life of Christ, I, 476).
