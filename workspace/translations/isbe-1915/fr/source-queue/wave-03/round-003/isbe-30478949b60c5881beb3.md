# CONFUSION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-fu'-zhun (bosheth, "shame, paleness," kelimmah, "blushing," tohu; akatastasia, sugchusis): In the Old Testament bosheth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 20:30⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 109:29⟧ the King James Version) and kelimmah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ps 44:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 30:3⟧) are the words most frequently translated "confusion"; tohu, "wastiness," "emptiness" is so translated (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Isa 24:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:34:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:41:29⟧), also qalon, "lightness," "contempt" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Job 10:15⟧ = ignominy, the American Standard Revised Version) and tebhel, "profanation" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Le 18:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:20:12⟧); ra`ash, "shaking," "trembling," rendered "confused" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Isa 9:5⟧ the King James Version; compare the Revised Version (British and American). Gr akatastasia, “instability” is translated "confusion" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:1Co 14:33⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Jas 3:16⟧); sugchusis, "a pouring out together" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:Ac 19:29⟧). In The Wisdom of Solomon 14:26, "changing of kind" (the King James Version) is rendered "confusion of sex." W. L. Walker
