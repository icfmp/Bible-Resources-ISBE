# ELIPHAL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
e-li'-fal, el'-i-fal ('eliphal, "God has judged"):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
Son of Ur, one of the mighty men of David's armies (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 11:35⟧). the Revised Version (British and American) in a footnote identifies him with Eliphelet, son of Ahasbai, the son of the Maachathite (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:2Sa 23:34⟧; ef Davis, Dict. of the Bible, under the word "Ur").
