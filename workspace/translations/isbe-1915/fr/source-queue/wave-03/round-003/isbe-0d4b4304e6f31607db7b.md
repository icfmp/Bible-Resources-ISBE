# CHERISH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
cher'-ish (cakhan; thalpo): Cakhan, "to act the friend," "to be useful," is translated "cherish" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 1:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:4⟧); thalpo, "to warm," "to make warm," "to foster" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eph 5:29⟧), said of the regard the husband should have for his wife, even as his own flesh which he "nourisheth and cherisheth, even as Christ also the church," and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Th 2:7⟧, of Paul amongst his converts, "as when a nurse cherisheth her own children."
