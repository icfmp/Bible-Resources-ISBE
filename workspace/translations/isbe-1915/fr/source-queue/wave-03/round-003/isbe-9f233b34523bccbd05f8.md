# EDUTH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
e'-duth (`edhuth, "testimony," a technical term for the Ten Commandments or for the Law):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 60⟧ title, "set to Shushan Eduth" (literally, "a lily (is) the testimony"); 80 title, "set to Shoshannim Eduth" (literally, "lilies (is) the testimony"). The Hebrew words appear to be intended to designate a melody by the first few words ordinarily associated with it.
