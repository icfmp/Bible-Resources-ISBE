# DEUEL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
du'-el, de-u'-el de`u'el, ("knowledge of God"): A Gadite, the father of Eliasaph, the representative of the tribe of Gad in the census-taking (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Nu 1:14⟧), in making the offering of the tribe at the dedication of the altar (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Nu 7:42⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:47⟧), and as leader of the host of the tribe of the children of Gad in the wilderness (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Nu 10:20⟧). Called Reuel in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Nu 2:14⟧, daleth (d) being confused with resh (r).
