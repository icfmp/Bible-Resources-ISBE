# DIGNITIES; DIGNITY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dig'-ni-tiz, dig'-ni-ti (Hebrew marom, se'eth, gedhullah): Rank or position, not nobility or austerity of personal character or bearing, is denoted by this word in its Old Testament occurrences (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 49:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Es 6:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ec 10:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Hab 1:7⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:2Pe 2:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Jude 1:8⟧, "dignities" (doxai) are angels, lofty spiritual beings, possible objects of blasphemy; compare the context in both passages.
