# EDGE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ej:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
Very frequently occurs in the phrase "the edge of the sword" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 10:28⟧, et al.) from the Hebrew peh, "lip," or saphah, "lip." ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ex 28:7⟧ and 39:4 read "ends," from qatsah, "end" (the King James Version "edge"), and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Jos 13:27⟧ has "uttermost part" for the same Hebrew word (the King James Version "edge"). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jer 31:29⟧ and ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Eze 18:2⟧, "The children's teeth are set on edge" (qahah, "to be blunt"), i.e. set hard one against another.
