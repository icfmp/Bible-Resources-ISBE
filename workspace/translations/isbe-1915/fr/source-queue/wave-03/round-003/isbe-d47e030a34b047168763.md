# COVET
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kuv'-et ('awah; zeloo, "to desire earnestly," "to set the heart and mind upon anything"): Used in two senses: good, simply to desire earnestly but legitimately. e.g. the King James Version ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Co 12:31⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:14:39⟧; bad, to desire unlawfully, or to secure illegitimately (batsa`; epithumeo, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ro 7:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:13:9⟧, etc.); hence, called "lust" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Mt 5:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Co 10:6⟧), "concupiscence" (the King James Version ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ro 7:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Col 3:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:10:tail⟧
