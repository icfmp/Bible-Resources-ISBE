# COMMIT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ko-mit': Used in two senses:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) "To give in charge" or "entrust": sim, "to put" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 5:8⟧); galal, "to roll" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ps 37:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Pr 16:3⟧); paqadh, "to give, in charge" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ps 31:5⟧ the King James Version; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Lu 23:46⟧); tithemi, "committed to us (the Revised Version, margin "placed in us") the word of reconciliation" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Co 5:19⟧); paratheke, "that which I have committed unto him" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:2Ti 1:12⟧; the Revised Version, margin "that which he hath committed unto me," Greek "my deposit"); "that which is committed unto thee" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:1Ti 6:20⟧, Greek "the deposit"); "that good thing," etc. (⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:2Ti 1:14⟧, Greek "the good deposit").
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:13 -->
(2) "To do or practice (evil)": prasso "commit such things" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Ro 1:32⟧, the Revised Version (British and American) "practice"; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Ro 2:2⟧). In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:1Jo 3:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:8⟧ "doeth sin" (poieo, the King James Version "committeth sin") shows that it is not committing a single sin that is in view, but sinful practice.
<!-- ISBE-END:13 -->
