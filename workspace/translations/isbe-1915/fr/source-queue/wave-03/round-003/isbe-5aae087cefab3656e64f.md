# DOLEFUL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dol'-fool ('oach, "howling"): The "doleful creatures" referred to in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 13:21⟧ are probably "jackals," although some have suggested "leopard," or "hyena." The older English Versions of the Bible gives "great owls." The word rendered "doleful lamentation" in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mic 2:4⟧ (niheyah) is simply a form of the word ordinarily translated "wailing" (nehi). Compare the King James Version margin.
