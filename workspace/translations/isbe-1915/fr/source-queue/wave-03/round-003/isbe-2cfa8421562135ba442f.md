# DARDA
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dar'-da (darda`, "pearl of wisdom"): One of the wise men to whom Solomon is compared (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 4:31⟧). He was either a son of Mahol (ibid.) or a son of Zerah, son of Judah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 2:6⟧, where the corresponding name in the same list is given as ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧DARA⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧). In rabbinic lore the name has been interpreted as dor dea, "the generation of knowledge"--the generation of the wilderness.
