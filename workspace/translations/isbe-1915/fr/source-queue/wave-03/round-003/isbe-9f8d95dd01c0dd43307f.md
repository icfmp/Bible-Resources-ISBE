# CONCEAL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sel' (parakalupto): Found but once in the New Testament (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 9:45⟧). The primary meaning is to cover by hanging something in front of the object hidden. The purpose of the one concealing is made prominent. There is, therefore, a reserve and studied progress in regard to the statement of facts, that is not always a suppression of truth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Pr 12:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:23⟧). God withholds more than He reveals (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Pr 25:2⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ps 97:2⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Ti 6:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧
