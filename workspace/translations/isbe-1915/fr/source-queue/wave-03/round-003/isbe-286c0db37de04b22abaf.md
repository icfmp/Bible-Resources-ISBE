# CONSUMPTION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sump'-shun (shachepheth, "wasting away"): One of the punishments which was to follow neglect or breach of the law. It may mean pulmonary consumption, which occurs frequently in Palestine; but from its association with fever in the texts, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Le 26:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:De 28:22⟧, it is more likely to be the much more common condition of wasting and emaciation from prolonged or often recurring attacks of malarial fever.
