# CHERITH, THE BROOK
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ke'-rith (nachal kerith; Cheimarrhous Chorrhath): The place where Elijah hid and was miraculously fed, after announcing the drought to Ahab (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Ki 17:3⟧). It is described as being "before," that is "east," of Jordan. It cannot therefore be identified with Wady el-Kelt, to the West of Jericho. The retreat must be sought in some recess of the Gilead uplands with which doubtless Elijah had been familiar in his earlier days.
