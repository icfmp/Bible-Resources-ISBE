# DEGENERATE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-jen'-er-at: Only in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 2:21⟧, where Judah is compared to a "noble vine" which it "turned into the degenerate branches of a foreign vine." It represents Hebrew curim = "stray" or "degenerate (shoots)," from cur = "to turn aside," especially to turn aside from the right path (Greek pikria, literally, "bitterness").
