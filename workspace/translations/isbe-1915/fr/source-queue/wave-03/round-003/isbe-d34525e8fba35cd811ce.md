# COPPERSMITH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kop'-er-smith (chalkeus): The word is found in New Testament once only, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ti 4:14⟧: "Alexander the coppersmith did (margin "showed") me much evil." As the Bible word rendered "copper" (see ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ezr 8:27⟧ the King James Version) is translated "brass" by the Revised Version (British and American), so the word here rendered "c." should be rendered "brazier," or "worker in brass."
