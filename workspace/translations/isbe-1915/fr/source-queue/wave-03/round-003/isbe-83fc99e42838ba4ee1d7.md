# CONTROVERSY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon'-tro-ver-si (ribh, "strife," "contention"; homologoumenos, "confessedly," "without controversy"): Used frequently of disputes among men (as ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 17:8⟧) and then transferred to the justice of God as directed against the sins of men. Thus we read of Yahweh's controversy with the nations (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jer 25:31⟧); with the inhabitants of the land (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ho 4:1⟧); with His people (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Mic 6:2⟧). "Without controversy" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Ti 3:16⟧), a positive rather than a negative expression, "by common consent," or better, "as unanimously confessed," introducing a quotation from a hymn or rhythmical confession of the early church.
