# COOL
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kool (ruach, "wind"; katapsucho, "to cool down"): "Cool of the day" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 3:8⟧, margin "wind"), when the evening breeze has tempered the heat of the day, enabling Orientals to walk abroad. "Cool my tongue" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 16:24⟧), a phrase reflecting the Jewish notion that Abraham had power to rescue his descendants from the fires of Hades.
