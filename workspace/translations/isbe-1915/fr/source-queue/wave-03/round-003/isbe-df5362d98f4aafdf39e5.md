# COMMUNE; COMMUNICATE; COMMUNICATION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
ko-mun', komun'-i-kat, ko-mu-ni-ka'-shun: To commune is to converse confidentially and sympathetically. It is represented in both Hebrew and Greek by several words literally signifying to speak (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Lu 6:11⟧, dialaleo; also ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 22:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ac 24:26⟧, homileo). To communicate is to impart something to another, so that it becomes common to giver and receiver. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ti 6:18⟧, "willing to communicate" (the Revised Version, margin "sympathize"), represents a single word koinonikoi, and refers to the habit of sharing with others either sympathy or property. the Revised Version (British and American) gives "companionships" for homiliai in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Co 15:33⟧ (the King James Version "communications").
