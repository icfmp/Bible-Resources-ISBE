# COVER; COVERING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kuv'-er, kuv'-er-ing: The translation of several Hebrew words. The covering of the ark (mikhceh, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 8:13⟧) was possibly the lid of a hatchway (compare Mitchell, World before Abraham, 215).
