# ELISABETH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
e-liz'-a-beth (Elisabet, Westcott and Hort Eleisdbet, from Heb 'elishebha` (Elisheba), "God is (my) oath," i.e. a worshipper of God):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
Wife of Zacharias the priest and mother of John the Baptist (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 1:5⟧ ff). Elisabeth herself was of priestly lineage and a "kinswoman" (the King James Version ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧COUSIN⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧, which see) of the Virgin Mary (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Lu 1:36⟧), of whose visit to Elisabeth a remarkable account is given in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Lu 1:39-56⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
