# COMPLAINING
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kom-plan'-ing (tsewachah, "cry," "outcry," siach, "meditation," "complaint"): tsewachah is translated "complaining" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 144:14⟧, the Revised Version (British and American)) "outcry," "no complaining (outcry) in our streets," i.e. "open places" where the people commonly assembled near the gate of the city (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:2Ch 32:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ne 8:1⟧); a picture of peace in the city (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Isa 24:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Jer 14:2⟧); some render "battlecry"; siach (the Revised Version (British and American) ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Pr 23:29⟧, the King James Version "babbling"), of the drunkard.
