<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:1 -->
# ELIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
e'-lim ('elim, "terebinths"; Aileim):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
The second encampment of the Israelites after crossing the Red Sea. It was a contrast to the previous camp called "Marah" because of the bitterness of the waters, for there "were twelve springs of water, and threescore and ten palm trees" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ex 15:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:16:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Nu 33:9⟧ f). The traditional site is an oasis in Wady Ghurundel, circa 63 miles from Suez.
