# CONFORM; CONFORMABLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-form', kon-form'-a-b'-l (summorphoo "to become or be like," or "of the same form"): Indicating an inner change of nature, working into the outward life (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ro 8:29⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Php 3:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:21⟧); while suschematizo, "fashioned according to" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Ro 12:21⟧ the Revised Version (British and American), the King James Version. "conformed"), refers to that which is external.
