# CONSULT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sult' (sha'al, malakh, ya`ats (Aramaic) ye`aT; sumbouleuomai):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) "To ask," "inquire," "seek advice." Ezekiel speaks of the king of Babylonian consulting the teraphim (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Eze 21:21⟧) and the Israelites were admonished to have noting to do with "a consulter with a familiar spirit" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:De 18:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:6 -->
See ⟦ISBE:OPEN:7⟧ASTROLOGY⟦ISBE:CLOSE:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:8⟧COMMUNION WITH DEMONS⟦ISBE:CLOSE:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:OPEN:9⟧DIVINATION⟦ISBE:CLOSE:9⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:6 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
(2) "To take counsel," "devise," "plan." The various officials of Babylon "consulted together to establish a royal statute" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Da 6:7⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Mt 26:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:10 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:13 -->
(3) "To deliberate with one's self," "make up one's mind." Nehemiah consulted with himself as to what might be done for Jerusalem (⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:Ne 5:7⟧). Jesus spoke of a king "consulting" (the King James Version) whether he be able to wage a war (⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:Lu 14:31⟧; the Revised Version (British and American) "take counsel").
