# CRUSE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kroos: A small earthen vessel or flask, usually for holding liquids: tsappachath; as water, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:1Sa 26:11⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Ki 19:6⟧; it being porous, the liquid is kept cool; also for holding oil, as in 1Ki l7:12,14,16.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:7 -->
In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:1Ki 14:3⟧ ("a cruse of honey") the word baqbuq, would be better rendered "bottle," doubtless deriving its name from the gurgling sound of issuing liquids. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:2Ki 2:20⟧ tselochith, is not a jar or flask, but a dish, or platter, for salt or other substances.
<!-- ISBE-END:7 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:10 -->
In the New Testament a small jar or vial, alabastron, "alabaster cruse" or flask, for holding ointment; not "box" as in the King James Version (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Mt 26:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:Mr 14:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:Lu 7:37⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:1Sa 10:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:2Ki 9:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:3⟧, where "box" in the King James Version is used for "vial" the Revised Version (British and American)).
