# CONTINUANCE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-tin'-u-ans: Not in the Revised Version (British and American); in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ps 139:16⟧ the King James Version, as an interpretation of Hebrew yamim, "days," treating of God's prevision, where the Revised Version (British and American) has: "They were all written, even the days that were ordained for me," i.e. all my days were in view, before one of them actually existed. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Isa 64:5⟧ the King James Version, for `olam, "of long time," the Revised Version (British and American); in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Ro 2:7⟧, for hupomone, "patience," the Revised Version (British and American), or still better, "steadlfastness," the Revised Version, margin.
