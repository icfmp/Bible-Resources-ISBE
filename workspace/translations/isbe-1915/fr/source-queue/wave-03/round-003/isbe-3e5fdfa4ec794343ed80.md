# DIVISION
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
di-vizh'-un: Used in English Versions of the Bible in the following senses:
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
(1) A separate body of people
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(a) of the tribal divisions of Israel (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 11:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:12:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:18:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:7:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:8 -->
(b) of sections of a tribe, "the divisions of Reuben" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jud 5:15⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:16⟧ the King James Version; but the Revised Version (British and American) rightly substitutes "the watercourses of Reuben"; in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Job 20:17⟧ the same word is rendered "rivers");
<!-- ISBE-END:8 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:12 -->
(c) of the (late) organization of priests and Levites into classes or families who ministered in the temple in rotation; translated "courses" generally in the King James Version, and always in the Revised Version (British and American) (⟦ISBE:KEEP:13:1Ch 24:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:13:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:14:26:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:14:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:15:12⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:15:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:16:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:16:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:17:Ne 11:36⟧; compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:18:2Ch 35:5⟧). Much prominence is given by the Chronicler to the 24 classes of priests, singers, and doorkeepers, who served in turns in the temple (compare ⟦ISBE:KEEP:19:Lu 1:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:19:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:20:8⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:20:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:12 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:21 -->
(3) In the New Testament, dissension, disunion, schism (⟦ISBE:KEEP:22:Lu 12:51⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:22:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:23:Ro 16:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:23:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:24:1Co 3:3⟧ the King James Version, omitted the Revised Version (British and American); ⟦ISBE:KEEP:25:1Co 1:10⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:25:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:26:11:18⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:26:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:27:Ga 5:20⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:27:tail⟧
