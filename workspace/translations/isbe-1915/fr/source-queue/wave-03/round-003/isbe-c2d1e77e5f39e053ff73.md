# CIRCLE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
sur'-k'-l: Is used with reference to the vault of the heavens (hugh) in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 40:22⟧, and in a similar sense in The Wisdom of Solomon 13:2 (Revised Version margin), "circle of stars" (kuklos astron). It is also used in the sense of surrounding territory, as in the expression "circle of Jordan" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ge 13:10⟧ the Revised Version, margin).
