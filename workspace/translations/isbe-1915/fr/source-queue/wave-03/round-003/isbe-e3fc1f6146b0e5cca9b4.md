# DODANIM
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
do'-da-nim (dodhanim, "leaders"): In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Ge 10:4⟧, the son of Javan, the son of Japheth. This would place the Dodanim among the Ioninns. The parallel passage ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Ch 1:7⟧, with the Septuagint and Samaritan Pentateuch, has, however, "Rodanim," which is probably the true reading. This identifies the people with the Rhodians (compare on ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eze 27:15⟧ under ⟦ISBE:OPEN:6⟧DEDAN⟦ISBE:CLOSE:6⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧
