# CROP
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(1) As noun the translation of mur'-ah (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Le 1:16⟧), which is the craw of a bird, especially of doves and pigeons, which had to be removed by the priest before he offered the birds as a burnt sacrifice.
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:4 -->
(2) As a verb it is (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Eze 17:4⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:22⟧) the translation of qaTaph, which has the meaning of "cutting off," "cutting down," "plucking."
<!-- ISBE-END:4 -->
