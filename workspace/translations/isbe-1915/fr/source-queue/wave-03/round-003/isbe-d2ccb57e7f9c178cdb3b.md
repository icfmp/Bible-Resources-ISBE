# CONVENT
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-vent': Found in the King James Version margin of ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Jer 49:19⟧: "Who will convent me in judgment?" and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jer 50:44⟧: "Who will convent me to plead?" The Hebrew term which is rendered convent is ya`adh, and it means to summon to a court, to call on to plead. Convent is obsolete, but it was formerly used, and meant to summon, or to call before a judge. Shakespeare used it several times. In King Henry VIII, Act V, he said, "The lords of the council hath commanded that the archbishop be convented to the council board."
