# DIG
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
(qur, "to dig", chathar; diorusso, "to dig through"): "I have digged (dug) and drunk strange waters" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ki 19:24⟧). In his campaigns on foreign soil, where the enemy had stopped up the watersprings, Sennacherib would at once dig fresh wells for his armies. "They dig through houses" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Job 24:16⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Mt 6:19⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:20⟧ margin). Walls of eastern houses are often made of mud or clay, and frequently have no windows; and as the threshold of a Syrian house is sacred, the thief breaks in through the wall (see Trumbull, The Threshold Covenant).
