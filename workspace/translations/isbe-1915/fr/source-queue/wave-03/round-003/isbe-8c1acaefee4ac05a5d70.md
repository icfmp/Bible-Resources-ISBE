# DOTE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
dot: "To dote" means either "to be weakminded" or "to be foolishly fond." In the latter sense it is employed in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Eze 23:5⟧ ff; in the former, in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jer 50:36⟧ the King James Version (the Revised Version (British and American) "shall become fools"); the King James Version Sirach 25:2 (the Revised Version (British and American) "lacking understanding"), and the King James Version ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:1Ti 6:4⟧ (the Revised Version, margin "to be sick"; the King James Version margin"a fool").
