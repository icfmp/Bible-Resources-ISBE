# CHINNERETH; CHINNEROTH
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kin'-e-reth, kin'-e-roth (kinnereth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:De 3:17⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:3:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Jos 19:35⟧, etc.)), (kinaroth; Codex Vaticanus, Kenereth, Codex Alexandrinus, Cheneroth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Jos 11:2⟧)): Taking the order in which the towns are mentioned, this city seems to have lain North of Rakkath (?Tiberias). It may have occupied the site of el-Mejdel, at the Southwest corner of the plain of Gennesaret. From this city the sea took its Old Testament name (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Nu 34:11⟧, etc.).
