# COW; KINE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kou, kin (baqar (compare Arabic baqar, "cow"); `eghlath baqar (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 7:21⟧); parah (compare Arabic furar, "young of a sheep, goat, or cow"); paroth `aloth (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:1Sa 6:7⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:10⟧), "milch kine," from `ul , "to suckle"; 'eleph): In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Am 4:1⟧, the term, "kine of Bashan," is applied to the voluptuous women of Samaria. In ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Ge 41:1-36⟧ is the narration of Pharaoh's dream of the seven fat and seven lean kine. In Isaiah's vision (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Isa 11:7⟧) we have: "And the cow and the bear shall feed; their young ones shall lie down together." Cows do not seem to have been sacrificed. The sacrifice of the kine that brought the ark back from the Philistines (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:1Sa 6:14⟧) was due to the exceptional circumstances.
