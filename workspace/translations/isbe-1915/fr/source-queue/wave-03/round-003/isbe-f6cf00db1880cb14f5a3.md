# EARTHEN VESSELS
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
urth'-'-n, (cheres, yetser; ostrakinos):
<!-- ISBE-END:2 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:3 -->
These vessels were heat-resisting and were used for cooking and for boiling clothes (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Le 6:28⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:11:33⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:14:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:6:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:50⟧). They were probably non-porous and took the place of the kidri or ma'ajin used in Syria today. A traveler in the interior of Palestine may still meet with the hospitality showed to David (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:2Sa 17:28⟧). The generous natives brought not only gifts of food but the necessary vessels in which to cook it. An earthen vessel was used to preserve a land deed (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Jer 32:14⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧
<!-- ISBE-END:3 -->
