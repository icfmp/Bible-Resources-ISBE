# CLAP
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
An emphatic expression of joy, "They clapped their hands (nakhah), and said, Long live (the King James Version "God save") the king" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:2Ki 11:12⟧); "Oh clap your hands (taqa`), all ye peoples" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 47:1⟧); or exultation (caphaq, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:La 2:15⟧; macha', ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Eze 25:6⟧; taqa`, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Na 3:19⟧); or repudiation (caphaq, ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Job 27:23⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:8:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:34:37⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧
