# COMELINESS; COMELY
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kum'-li-nes, kum'-li: Cognate with "becoming," namely, what is suitable, graceful, handsome. The servant of Yahweh in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Isa 53:2⟧ is without "comeliness" (hadhar, "honor"), i.e. there is in his appearance nothing attractive, while he is bowed beneath man's sin. "Praise is comely" (na'wah, f. of na'weh; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Ps 33:1⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:147:1⟧), i.e. suitable or befitting "for the righteous," and, therefore, an honor and glory; "uncomely parts," aschemona (⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:1Co 12:23⟧), namely, less honorable. See also ⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:1Sa 16:18⟧, "a comely person"; ⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:So 6:4⟧, "comely as Jerusalem." etc.
