# CONSIDER
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
kon-sid'-er: In the New Testament the force of the word is brought out most vividly in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Mt 6:26⟧ (katamanthano), where it means to "examine closely," as though the observer had to bend down for this purpose, and in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Lu 12:27⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:4:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:5:Heb 10:24⟧ (katanoeo, to "observe well"), while in ⟦ISBE:KEEP:6:Heb 13:7⟧ the anatheoreo, "look up toward" or "look again at" is consistent with the reverential regard commended in the context. Used in the Old Testament for a variety of Hebrew terms, signifying inspecting (⟦ISBE:KEEP:7:Pr 31:16⟧), examining (⟦ISBE:KEEP:8:Le 13:13⟧), giving serious thoughts to (⟦ISBE:KEEP:9:Ps 77:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:9:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:10:Isa 1:3⟧), it often means little more than "see" or "behold" (⟦ISBE:KEEP:11:Ps 8:3⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:11:tail⟧⟦ISBE:KEEP:12:9:13⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:12:tail⟧
