# DEBATE
<!-- ISBE-END:1 -->

<!-- ISBE-BLOCK:2 -->
de-bat': This word is used only once in the Revised Version (British and American) (⟦ISBE:KEEP:3:Pr 25:9⟧). It evidently refers to the settling of a difficulty with a neighbor, and anticipates ⟦ISBE:KEEP:4:Mt 18:15⟧. It argues for and shows the advantage of private, peaceable settlement of difficulties. Compare Ecclesiasticus 28:9, and see ⟦ISBE:OPEN:5⟧MAKEBATES⟦ISBE:CLOSE:5⟧⟦ISBE:FIX:5:tail⟧
